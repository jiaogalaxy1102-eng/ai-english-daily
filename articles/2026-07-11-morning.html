Photos of purported cease and desist letters from the company have gone viral, but Flock says it didn’t send them.

網傳該公司寄發停止侵權通知函的照片，但 Flock 表示並非其所寄。

On Thursday, the Instagram account for a lecture series in Newport Beach, CA posted a photo of what appeared to be a cease and desist letter from the surveillance technology company Flock Safety. Flock has received significant backlash over its technology and work with law enforcement agencies, and this letter kicked off yet another wave of criticism against the company for allegedly trying to shut down discussions of its work.

週四，加州新港灘一個講座系列的 Instagram 帳號發布了一張照片，看起來像是來自監視技術公司 Flock Safety 的停止侵權通知函。Flock 因其技術以及與執法機構的合作而面臨巨大的輿論反彈，而這封信引發了另一波對該公司的批評，指責其試圖封殺對其工作的討論。

“WE WILL NOT BE SILENCED,” the group, The Saturday Salon, wrote inthe Instagram post, which has more than 3,000 likes. A separate post about the letter on Bluesky hasmore than 360 reposts. The text of the letter demands that the group stop hosting conversations about Flock’s surveillance technology. In a DM from The Saturday Salon’s Instagram account, Schuyler Lifschultz tellsThe Vergethat it “found this letter taped to our front door.”

「我們不會沈默，」名為「週六沙龍」的團體在 Instagram 貼文中寫道，該貼文已獲得超過 3,000 個讚。Bluesky 上另一篇關於此信函的貼文也有超過 360 次轉發。信件內容要求該團體停止主辦關於 Flock 監視技術的對話。在來自「週六沙龍」Instagram 帳號的私訊中，Schuyler Lifschultz 告訴《The Verge》，這封信是「貼在我們前門發現的」。

But Flock denies that it sent the letter, with chief strategy officer Rahul Sidhu citing it as an example of a mass disinformation campaign against Flock. “Flock never sent this letter, these people made it up (with a forged signature) to try to manipulate people,”Sidhu says. “We are pro-democracy. People SHOULD have discussions and lectures like this.”

但 Flock 否認寄出該信，其首席策略長 Rahul Sidhu 稱其為針對 Flock 的大規模虛假訊息運動的一個案例。「Flock 從未寄出這封信，這些人捏造了它（帶有偽造簽名）試圖操縱大眾，」Sidhu 表示。「我們支持民主。人們應該進行這樣的討論和講座。」

In a statement toThe Verge, Flock’s chief legal officer, Dan Haley, says that Flock is aware of “at least two forged” letters and that “these letters did not come from me or from anyone at Flock.” Here is Haley’s full statement:

在給《The Verge》的聲明中，Flock 的首席法務長 Dan Haley 表示，Flock 已知悉「至少兩封偽造的」信函，且「這些信件並非出自我也非出自 Flock 的任何人」。以下是 Haley 的完整聲明：

We’re aware of at least two forged letters circulating on the internet, including this one, that purport to be cease-and-desist letters from our legal department. To be clear: these letters did not come from me or from anyone at Flock.

我們知悉網路上流傳著至少兩封偽造信函，包括這一封，其聲稱是來自我們法律部門的停止侵權通知函。澄清一下：這些信件並非出自我也非出自 Flock 的任何人。

Flock welcomes and encourages public debate about our technology. We have not and would not seek to discourage, prevent, or prohibit such discussion and debate. In fact, we would be happy to participate in any such discussions the group in question might host in the future.

Flock 歡迎並鼓勵大眾對我們的技術進行辯論。我們現在和未來都不會尋求阻止、防止或禁止此類討論與辯論。事實上，我們很樂意參與該團體未來可能舉辦的任何此類討論。

Looking closely at the photo of the letter in The Saturday Salon’s post reveals some dodgy details. Haley’s title in the letter is “Head of Legal Affairs Division,” but his actual title is chief legal officer.The Vergealso emailed Haley’s purported email address on the letter but received a bounceback.

仔細觀察「週六沙龍」貼文中的信件照片，會發現一些可疑細節。Haley 在信中的職稱為「法律事務部負責人」，但其正式職稱是首席法務長。《The Verge》也向信中提供的 Haley 電子郵件地址發送郵件，但遭退信。

Lifschultz tellsThe Vergethat The Saturday Salon would “love to have somebody from Flock come in and tell their views and give a lecture. We are politically neutral and not a business.”

Lifschultz 告訴《The Verge》，「週六沙龍」會「很樂意邀請 Flock 的人來分享觀點並舉辦講座。我們在政治上保持中立，並非營利事業。」

One of the other apparently forged letters Flock is aware of was shared inan Instagram postfrom musician Noah Orion. On his website, Orion hasa shop listing for “Fuck Flock” stickers, and the cease and desist letter in the photo says that Orion has engaged in conduct “involving the unauthorized dissemination” of visual materials in a way that encourages Orion’s fans to “claim and place stickers that constitute a rude and unusual manner towards our company and association.”

Flock 知悉的另一封顯然是偽造的信件，是由音樂家 Noah Orion 在 Instagram 貼文中分享的。在 Orion 的網站上，他有一個「Fuck Flock」貼紙的商店列表，而照片中的停止侵權通知函稱，Orion 從事了「未經授權傳播」視覺素材的行為，並以此鼓勵粉絲「索取並張貼對我們公司及協會構成粗魯且異常行為的貼紙」。

This letter also has some unusual characteristics, such as identifying the company as Flock Cameras and Flock incorporated [sic]. The letter in the photo says that failure to comply with the purported cease and desist “may result in Flock Group inc. [sic] to persecute you”, which is both an awkwardly worded phrase and incorrectly uses “persecute” instead of “prosecute.” Orion didn’t immediately reply to a request for comment.

這封信也有一些不尋常的特徵，例如將公司識別為 Flock Cameras 和 Flock incorporated [原文如此]。照片中的信函稱，若不遵守所謂的停止侵權通知，「可能導致 Flock Group inc. [原文如此] 對你進行迫害 (persecute)」，這既是一個措辭笨拙的短語，也錯誤地使用了「persecute」而非「起訴 (prosecute)」。Orion 並未立即回覆評論請求。
