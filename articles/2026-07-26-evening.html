Basic Business? Platinum Pro? Premium Plus? Airlines have a naming problem—here’s how to fix it

2026-07-26  ·  原文連結

In early July, Delta Airlines announced that it would begin offering a new category of fares for its Delta First, Delta Premium Select, and Delta One seat tiers. The Delta One option came with a name that felt like an oxymoron: “Basic Business.”

7 月初，達美航空宣布將開始為其達美頭等艙、達美尊尚客艙及達美至臻商務艙（Delta One）座位級別提供新的票價類別。其中「達美至臻」的選項包含了一個感覺像是矛盾修辭的名稱：「基礎商務艙」。

According to Delta’s press release on the move, these “Basic” ticket options allow customers access to some premium onboard experiences, but at a lower price point and with some trade-offs—like reduced checked bag allowance and lower mileage earned. Basic Business, for example, is described as an “entry-level tier for Delta One,” which essentially gives passengers the in-flight Delta One experience without access to Delta One check-in or the company’s swanky Delta One lounges.

根據達美航空關於此舉的新聞稿，這些「基礎」票價選項允許客戶享受一些優質的機上體驗，但價格較低且需做出一些權衡——例如減少託運行李額度和降低里程累積。例如，「基礎商務艙」被描述為「達美至臻的入門級別」，這基本上是讓乘客體驗達美至臻的機上服務，但無法使用達美至臻的報到櫃檯或該公司豪華的達美至臻貴賓室。

Delta isn’t the first airline to experiment with the concept of a “basic” business tier. Earlier this year, United introduced a new “Base” fare for its top “Polaris” ticket that strips away several of the regular Polaris fare’s perks (like access to the Polaris lounge). As major airlines’ business models increasingly prioritize new premium offerings geared toward more affluent consumers, as well as emphasize added fees, they’re essentially driving profits by chopping the ticket-buying process into smaller and smaller chunks.

達美並非第一家嘗試「基礎」商務艙概念的航空公司。今年早些時候，聯合航空為其頂級的「Polaris」機票推出了新的「基礎（Base）」票價，剝奪了多項常規 Polaris 票價的福利（例如進入 Polaris 貴賓室的權限）。隨著主要航空公司的商業模式日益優先考慮針對更富裕消費者的全新高端產品，並強調增加費用，他們實際上正透過將購票過程切成越來越小的區塊來獲取利潤。

On social media, the reaction to Delta’s new fares has been swift, with commenters critiquing both the overall concept and the head-scratching Basic Business naming convention. For many travelers, the move is emphasizing how convoluted airline tier names have become.

在社群媒體上，對達美新票價的反應非常迅速，評論者批評了整體概念和令人費解的「基礎商務艙」命名慣例。對於許多旅客來說，此舉正突顯了航空分級名稱已變得多麼複雜。

According to multiple brand naming experts who spoke with Fast Company, there’s a reason that so many major airlines are making the same mistakes: They’re all playing a big game of telephone within one of the most risk-averse industries in the world, and it ultimately results in a lose-lose for everyone.

根據多位接受《Fast Company》採訪的品牌命名專家表示，這麼多主要航空公司犯下同樣錯誤是有原因的：他們都在一個全球最規避風險的產業中玩著一場大型的傳聲筒遊戲，而這最終導致了雙輸的局面。

“If Delta could charge you for using the lavatory they would”

「如果達美航空能為使用洗手間收費，他們也會這麼做」

The most common reactions to Delta’s new Basic Business class have been confusion, skepticism, and frustration.

對於達美航空新的「基礎商務艙」最常見的反應是困惑、懷疑和沮喪。

One post in the subreddit r/delta circulating the news has amassed more than 200 comments. The top comment reads, “Per usual nothing delta changes actually improves the experience for its patrons. They will segment business and first class by stripping some perks from it. Which I believe will also lead to a cascading effect of more segmenting of classes.” Another user responded to this sentiment, “If delta could charge you for using the lavatory they would.”

Reddit 的 r/delta 版塊中一篇流傳該新聞的貼文已累積了超過 200 條評論。最熱門的評論寫道：「像往常一樣，達美的任何改動都沒有真正改善乘客的體驗。他們會透過剝奪福利來細分商務艙和頭等艙。我相信這也將導致更多艙位細分的連鎖效應。」另一位用戶對此觀點回應道：「如果達美能為使用洗手間收費，他們也會這麼做。」
