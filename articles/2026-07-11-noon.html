The funky font on Norway’s World Cup jersey? It’s inspired by Nordic runes

When Norway’s national soccer team first experimented with kits that used a font inspired by the runic alphabet, an ancient Scandinavian written language used by Vikings, it was too hard to read.

當挪威國家足球隊首次嘗試使用受盧恩字母（維京人使用的古代斯堪地那維亞文字）啟發的字體來製作球衣時，該字體太難辨認了。

Characters onNorway’s 2024 jerseyswere jagged, sharp, and not easily discernible. On the country’s home kits, they were beveled and even more difficult to make out, baffling plenty of first-time viewers. Plus, they didn’t adhere to FIFA’s rules for thetypographyof World Cup kits to be “clearly legible and distinguishable from a distance” for players, officials, spectators, and the media.

挪威 2024 年球衣上的字符呈鋸齒狀、鋒利且不易辨識。在該國的主場球衣上，這些字符帶有斜角，甚至更難辨認，讓許多初次觀看的觀眾感到困惑。此外，它們不符合國際足總 (FIFA) 對世界盃球衣字體 (typography) 的規定，即字體必須「從遠處看也能清晰易讀且易於區分」，以便球員、裁判、觀眾和媒體辨識。

Sofor the 2026 World Cup, Norway’s first appearance in the tournament since 1998, Nike, which makes their kits, helped the team design a new font that balances readability with heritage.

因此，為了 2026 年世界盃（挪威自 1998 年以來首次進入該項賽事），球衣製造商 Nike 協助球隊設計了一種在可讀性與文化傳承 (heritage) 之間取得平衡的新字體。

The typeface used for Norway’s uniforms is called Taakeferd, a Norwegian word meaning “journey through the fog.” It nods to the country’s return to the World Cup for the first time in 28 years.

挪威球衣使用的字體名為「Taakeferd」，這是一個挪威詞彙，意為「穿越迷霧的旅程」。這象徵著該國 28 年來首次重返世界盃。

FIFA regulations also specify the sizing and placement of player names and numberson uniforms, and they must be in Latin characters, though they can use diacritical marks, like accents and umlauts.

FIFA 的規定還具體說明了球員姓名和號碼在球衣上的尺寸和放置位置，且必須使用拉丁字符，不過可以使用變音符號，如重音符號和分音符。

With Norway headed to the quarterfinals Saturday against England, it’s clear Taakeferd passed the test. The typeface is composed of vertical or diagonal lines, which makes for distinctive, triangular shapes on curved Latin letterform in letters like B and R.

隨著挪威在週六對陣英格蘭的八強賽中晉級，顯然 Taakeferd 通過了測試。該字體由垂直或對角線組成，在像 B 和 R 這樣彎曲的拉丁字母形狀上形成了獨特的三角形。

The Nike design team designed Taakeferd in partnership with the Norwegian Football Federation; the goal was to create kits that felt unmistakably Norwegian.

Nike 設計團隊與挪威足球協會合作設計了 Taakeferd；其目標是打造出具有鮮明挪威風格的球衣。
