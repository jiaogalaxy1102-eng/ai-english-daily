The ‘harmony machine’ will be out by the end of the year for ‘around £550.’

這台「和聲機器」將在年底前上市，售價約為 550 英鎊。

After setting the music gear corner of the internet on fire back in 2023 with thefirst glimpseat theNopia, creators Martin Grieco and Rocío Gal are almost ready to bring it to market. The duo brought it to theMusicRadaroffices for an in-depth first look and revealed that it will be launching in “a couple of months” for around £550.

在 2023 年首度亮相並讓網路上的音樂設備圈為之瘋狂後，創作者 Martin Grieco 和 Rocío Gal 已準備好將 Nopia 推向市場。這對搭檔帶著它來到 MusicRadar 辦公室進行深入的首度預覽，並透露它將在「幾個月內」上市，售價約為 550 英鎊。

Nopia is built around harmonic interplay in a unique way. Rather than a few knobs and a keyboard controlling a single synth patch, it blends multiple modules — keys, bass, arp, and pad — into a single performance, not unlike a drumlessgroovebox. There’s a one-octave keyboard called the Chord Builder, a 12-button Tonal Selector, and an Extensions Dial that dictate the key and voicing of the chords. The idea is to let you play complex harmonies with just a finger or two.

Nopia 以獨特的和聲互動方式打造。它不是透過幾個旋鈕和鍵盤來控制單一合成器音色，而是將多個模組（琴鍵、貝斯、琶音和背景音）融合進單一演奏中，與不含鼓組的節奏機（groovebox）類似。機身配備一個單八度的「和弦構建器」鍵盤、一個 12 鍵的「音調選擇器」以及一個「擴展撥盤」，用來決定和弦的調性和聲部配置。其理念是讓你僅用一兩根手指就能彈奏複雜的和聲。

Additional performance features include a strum plate in the top-right corner for plucking specific notes from a chord and a slider for full chord pitch bends.

額外的演奏功能包括位於右上角、用於撥動和弦中特定音符的「撥弦板」，以及用於全和弦音高彎曲的滑動條。

In addition to the virtual analog and sample-based synth engines, there are basic effects like delay, reverb, tape emulation, and beat repeat, as well as a ton of connectivity options, including per-module MIDI output for controlling other instruments with Nopia’s harmonic engine.

除了虛擬類比和基於取樣的合成引擎外，還有延遲、殘響、磁帶模擬和節拍重複等基本效果，以及大量的連接選項，包括每模組 MIDI 輸出，以便使用 Nopia 的和聲引擎來控制其他樂器。
