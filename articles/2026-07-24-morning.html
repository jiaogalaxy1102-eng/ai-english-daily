Drawing from walks, photographs and Google Maps, the artist’s slow, sequential process turns familiar places into uncanny scenes.

藝術家透過散步、攝影和 Google 地圖，以緩慢且連貫的過程將熟悉的地點轉化為不可思議的場景。

Joanna Blémont’s paintings feel like those quiet movie scenes where you’re made to dwell a little longer on a shot in anticipation of what’s about to happen next. The apprehension felt in these artworks leaves you with the impression that there is something being hidden that’s on the edge of being felt or seen.

Joanna Blémont 的畫作給人的感覺就像是那些安靜的電影畫面，讓你被迫在一個鏡頭前停留稍久，期待接下來會發生什麼。這些作品中所感受到的憂慮，讓你產生一種印象，彷彿有什麼東西隱藏在即將被感受或看見的邊緣。

Although familiar, these intricate and eerie illustrated moments aren’t of any particular place, and this is intentional. “I tend to draw spaces which can feel familiar to a wider group of people, I approach this by removing place-specific markers,” Joanna says. Instead of translating a particular landscape or space into a watercolour work, the artist’s creative process consists of going on walks to take reference photographs of a range of different environments and using Google Maps to explore locations near and far to take snippets from for inspiration – a hangover from illustrating in lockdown during the pandemic. Joanna is drawn to the “unreality” brought about by this inconclusive mix of spaces and forms: “I think the architecture in some pieces will always give away the vague location. The figures in my work are often silhouettes or shadows, they’re symbolic of a presence more than a person.”

雖然感覺熟悉，但這些複雜且怪誕的插畫瞬間並不屬於任何特定地點，而這是刻意為之的。Joanna 表示：「我傾向於描繪能讓更廣泛人群感到熟悉的空間，我透過移除特定地點的標記來達成這一點。」藝術家的創作過程並非將特定的景觀或空間轉化為水彩作品，而是透過散步來拍攝各種不同環境的參考照片，並利用 Google 地圖探索遠近的地理位置，從中擷取片段作為靈感——這是大流行期間居家隔離時插畫創作留下的習慣。Joanna 被這種空間與形式的不確定組合所帶來的「非現實感」所吸引：「我認為某些作品中的建築總是會透露出模糊的地點。我作品中的人物通常是剪影或影子，他們象徵的是一種存在，而非具體的人。」

Painting in sequences, Joanna explores one scene through multiple lenses. Sometimes these sequences start as watercolour or pencil marks and then turn into Risograph prints, comics or publications – like her latest zine Comatose, an artist book that set out to explore the theme of “the transition between wakefulness and sleep”, Joanna says. More recently the artist produced an animated sequence of over 2,000 watercolour frames with a whole team of painters for fashion house Loewe’s 180 year anniversary campaign, and the result is something out of this world. Although labour intensive, what Joanna enjoys most is “the slow pace required to work with these mediums”, she ends. “I feel I make better work this way.”

Joanna 以序列化的方式繪畫，透過多重鏡頭探索同一個場景。有時這些序列始於水彩或鉛筆筆觸，然後轉化為孔版印刷（Risograph）、漫畫或出版品——例如她最新的小誌《Comatose》，Joanna 說這是一本旨在探索「清醒與睡眠之間的過渡」主題的藝術書。最近，這位藝術家與一整個畫家團隊為時尚品牌 Loewe 的 180 週年活動製作了一個包含 2,000 多個水彩畫格的動畫序列，其結果令人驚艷。雖然這項工作極耗體力，但 Joanna 最享受的是「使用這些媒介工作所需的緩慢步調」，她最後說道：「我覺得我用這種方式能做出更好的作品。」
