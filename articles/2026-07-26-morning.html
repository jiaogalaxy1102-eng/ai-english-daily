It’s just the latest victim of RAMageddon.

這只是記憶體浩劫 (RAMageddon) 的最新受害者。

Google’s Vice President of Devices and Services, Shakil Barkat, all but confirmed in an interview with 9to5 Google that its next Pixel phone would cost more than the Pixel 10. Considering the ongoing RAM supply issues due to the explosion of AI data centers, the rumored price hike is not a complete surprise. Companies from Apple to Nintendo, Microsoft to Roku, have all raised their prices response to">in response to the soaring cost of memory.

Google 裝置與服務副總裁 Shakil Barkat 在接受《9to5 Google》採訪時，幾乎證實了其下一款 Pixel 手機的價格將高於 Pixel 10。考量到 AI 數據中心激增導致的記憶體供應持續短缺，傳聞中的價格上漲並不完全令人意外。從 Apple 到任天堂、微軟到 Roku，各大公司都因記憶體成本飆升而調漲了價格。

Barkat said that Google had “shielded our consumers from supply fluctuations for as long as possible,” but that the “economics have fundamentally shifted and we’re not immune to that.” He went on to tell 9to5 that the Pixel lineup would see pricing “adjustments,” accordingly, that “will be rolled out dynamically to match supply realities.”

Barkat 表示，Google 已「盡可能長時間地保護我們的消費者免受供應波動的影響」，但「經濟環境已發生根本性轉變，我們也無法倖免」。他接著告訴 9to5，Pixel 系列將會進行相應的定價「調整」，並將「動態推行以符合供應現實」。

The base model Pixel 11 is expected to cost $899, but would come with 256GB of storage, instead of 128GB. It’s also rumored that the Pixel 11 Pro could go from 16GB of RAM to 12GB. We won’t have to wait too long to find out for sure, though. Google’s scheduled to announce the next generation of Pixel devices on August 12th.

基本款 Pixel 11 的售價預計為 899 美元，但儲存空間將從 128GB 增加到 256GB。另有傳言稱 Pixel 11 Pro 的記憶體可能會從 16GB 降至 12GB。不過，我們不需要等太久就能確定。Google 預計於 8 月 12 日發表下一代 Pixel 裝置。
