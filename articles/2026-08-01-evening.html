D’Wayne Edwards, legacy sneaker designer at Nike and president of Pensole Lewis College, has been studying Bruce Lee since he was in high school. “I thought he was this guy who did karate,” says Edwards. “But he was an incredible philosopher.” Having received his design education entirely on the job (first at Skechers and then at Nike), Edwards says he gleaned so much of his approach to design from watching and reading Lee’s work. “He doesn’t have a fighting style,” says Edwards. “His fighting style is whatever his opponent forces him to do. That’s how I look at design.”

D’Wayne Edwards，曾任職於 Nike 的傳奇球鞋設計師，現任 Pensole Lewis 學院院長，自高中以來便一直在鑽研李小龍。「我原以為他只是一個練空手道的人，」Edwards 說。「但他是一位不可思議的哲學家。」Edwards 的設計教育完全是在實務工作中習得的（先是在 Skechers，後來在 Nike），他說他從觀看和閱讀李小龍的作品中獲得了許多設計方法。「他沒有特定的格鬥風格，」Edwards 說。「他的格鬥風格取決於對手逼他做出的任何應對。這就是我看待設計的方式。」

Edwards says his design philosophy is not about him or his approach, but about the task in front of him: “I focus on becoming the creative or the designer for the problem I’m trying to solve.”

Edwards 說他的設計哲學不在於他自己或他的方法，而在於他面前的任務：「我專注於針對我試圖解決的問題，讓自己成為相應的創意人或設計師。」

He is now leaning on his career in product design to reshape the state and future of design education at Detroit’s Pensole Lewis College of Business & Design, the only design-focused HBCU, and what Edwards hopes sets the standard for a design education of the future. Tethered to these philosophies and his lifelong love of art, Edwards frames design in problem-solving, business, and education. Here are his rules.

他現在正運用他在產品設計領域的職業生涯，重塑底特律 Pensole Lewis 商務與設計學院（底特律唯一的設計導向「歷史性非裔大學」(HBCU)）的設計教育現狀與未來，Edwards 希望這能為未來的設計教育樹立標準。結合這些哲學和他對藝術終身的熱愛，Edwards 將設計框架在問題解決、商業和教育之中。以下是他的準則。

Break your life into chapters. I started out as an artist—I could draw anything I saw. Then I discovered design, and I used my artistic ability to become a designer. But the thinking was completely different; now I have to imagine things and make them real. I then started to understand that the human brain dictates my output. That’s when I transitioned from being a designer into being a creative.

將人生劃分為不同的篇章。我最初是一名藝術家——我能畫出我看到的任何東西。後來我發現了設計，並運用我的藝術能力成為一名設計師。但思考方式完全不同；現在我必須想像事物並讓它們成真。接著我開始明白，人類的大腦主宰著我的產出。那是當我從設計師轉變為創意人的時候。

Make creativity a daily exercise. We’re all creative. I look at creativity as an everyday action of walking down the street. How am I going to get past this pothole? I have to make a decision. Embracing everything through a lens of creativity is a much more fun, and more open, way to experience life. Wouldn’t you want a creative accountant? A creative lawyer? I would want to put “creative” in front of any person or word that can make my life better. You won’t get the same results. You won’t get the same static thing.

將創意視為每日的練習。我們每個人都富有創意。我將創意視為走在街上的日常行為。我要如何避開這個坑洞？我必須做出決定。透過創意的鏡頭去擁抱一切，是體驗生活的一種更有趣、更開放的方式。難道你不想找個有創意的會計師嗎？或是有創意的律師？我希望能將「創意」放在任何能讓我的生活變得更好的職稱或詞語之前。你不會得到相同的結果，也不會得到同樣停滯不前的事物。

To best honor the task in front of you, avoid a design style. I have a visual design language that comes through when you see my work. If there is a philosophy, I’d say focus on becoming the creative or the designer for the problem you’re trying to solve.

為了最好地致敬你面前的任務，避免特定的設計風格。我有一套視覺設計語言，當你看到我的作品時就會顯現出來。如果有什麼哲學的話，我會說要專注於針對你試圖解決的問題，成為那樣的創意人或設計師。

Look at other industries for insight. I started meeting other types of designers or creatives, who live in different places and have a different approach to life. I developed creative circles that I can tap into for conversations, because something will come out of it. These people are energy givers. You are way better than before you talked to them. Then, there are energy takers, and there are way more of them in the world than givers. You have to be disciplined with who you allow in your life.

尋求其他行業的見解。我開始結識其他類型的設計師或創意人，他們住在不同的地方，有著不同的生活方式。我建立了自己的創意圈，可以從中汲取對話靈感，因為對話中總會產生一些火花。這些人是能量的給予者。在與他們交談後，你會感覺比以前好得多。然而，世界上也有能量的掠奪者，而且他們比給予者多得多。你必須嚴格約束自己允許誰進入你的生活。
