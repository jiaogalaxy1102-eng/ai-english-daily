The Austrian photographer captures overlooked details and fleeting moments, making you incredibly curious about what’s going on just out of frame.

這位奧地利攝影師捕捉被忽視的細節與稍縱即逝的瞬間，讓你對畫框之外發生的事情感到無比好奇。

The title of Kurt Bauer’s self-published portfolio of works from the past six years is Turista per Sempre (Forever a Tourist) – a phrase that quite fittingly reflects the Austrian photographer’s approach: “looking at places with the mindset of a visitor, whether far from home or just around the corner”, the photographer says. For Kurt, the art of image making has always been a way to stay attentive to what unfolds around him and to notice details that not everyone records. “What interests me most is the possibility of seeing the ordinary anew,” he says. “I often find myself returning to subjects that are easy to overlook – small traces of human presence, objects shaped by time, or moments when light briefly transforms an everyday scene into something unexpected.”

庫爾特·鮑爾（Kurt Bauer）自出版的過去六年作品集標題為《永遠的遊客》（Turista per Sempre）——這個短語恰如其分地反映了這位奧地利攝影師的方法：「無論是遠離家鄉還是就在轉角處，都以遊客的心態觀察地方，」攝影師說。對庫爾特而言，影像創作藝術一直是他保持對周遭事物關注並注意到那些並非每個人都會記錄的細節的一種方式。「我最感興趣的是重新審視平凡事物的可能性，」他說。「我經常發現自己回到那些容易被忽視的主題——人類存在的微小痕跡、受時間形塑的物體，或是光線短暫地將日常場景轉化為意想不到之物的時刻。」

Rather than describing a place directly, Kurt’s still life scenes tend to feel like fragments of something larger at play. They do one thing very well – they make you incredibly curious about what’s sitting just beyond the frame. To achieve such a level of intrigue, Kurt is able to capture a story trapped somewhat within a singular moment: an untouched drink left on a table; an abandoned towel by the pool; or the remains of someone’s dinner spilt down a style of shoe that might tell us a lot about their character. His background in art direction and visual storytelling comes through in these seemingly pre-planned compositions – transferring his carefully constructed studio images to these quick observations made on the move.

庫爾特的靜物場景並非直接描述一個地方，而是傾向於讓人感覺像是某個更宏大運作中的片段。它們把一件事做得非常好——讓妳對畫框之外的事物感到極度好奇。為了達到這種引人入勝的程度，庫爾特能夠捕捉到困在單一瞬間內的故事：桌上沒動過的飲料、泳池旁遺棄的毛巾，或是某人晚餐殘渣灑在一種可能透露出其性格的鞋款上。他在藝術指導和視覺敘事方面的背景體現在這些看似預先規劃的構圖中——將他精心設計的棚拍影像轉化為這些在行進中捕捉的快速觀察。

“Regardless of the setting,” he says, “I’m interested in paying close attention and allowing room for chance. I’m drawn to photographs that leave room for ambiguity and allow memory, observation and imagination to overlap.” It’s through these movie-like shots that he aims to frame images that “tell me more about a place than a postcard view ever could,” he ends.

「無論環境如何，」他說，「我都對保持密切關注並為偶然留出空間感興趣。我被那些留有模糊空間，並讓記憶、觀察與想像力重疊的照片所吸引。」正是通過這些電影般的鏡頭，他旨在框取那些「比明信片風景更能告訴我關於一個地方的故事」的影像，他最後說道。
