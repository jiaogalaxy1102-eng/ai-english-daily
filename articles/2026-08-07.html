The US health department is working to shut down a Kentucky-based organ procurement organization that made headlines over a 2021 incident in which it was accused of trying to harvest a man’s organs while he was still alive.

美國衛生部門正致力於關閉一家總部位於肯塔基州的器官採集機構，該機構因 2021 年的一起事件而成為頭條新聞，當時它被指控試圖在一名男子仍活著時摘取其器官。

In an announcement Wednesday, the Department of Health and Human Services said it had begun the process to decertify the organization, Network for Hope (NFH). Losing its certification would effectively shutter the organization, which serves Kentucky as well as parts of Indiana, Ohio, and West Virginia. If decertified, another organization will be appointed to coordinate donations in the region.

在週三的一份公告中，美國衛生與公共服務部 (HHS) 表示已開始撤銷該機構「希望網路」(NFH) 認證的程序。失去認證將有效地使該機構倒閉，該機構服務於肯塔基州以及印第安納州、俄亥俄州和西維吉尼亞州的部分地區。如果認證被撤銷，將指派另一個組織來協調該地區的捐贈事宜。

In a statement, NFH said it “strongly disagrees” with the HHS’s finding and will appeal the decertification decision.

在一份聲明中，NFH 表示它「強烈反對」HHS 的調查結果，並將對撤銷認證的決定提出上訴。

HHS said its decision stems from two federal investigations of NFH that found “persistent patient safety failures,” despite extensive oversight and opportunities to correct failures. The two investigations were carried out separately by the HHS’s Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services and the Health Resources and Services Administration (HRSA).

HHS 表示，其決定源於對 NFH 的兩項聯邦調查，儘管有廣泛的監督和糾正缺失的機會，但調查仍發現其存在「持續性的病人安全失敗」。這兩項調查分別由 HHS 屬下的聯邦醫療保險和聯邦醫療補助服務中心，以及衛生資源和服務管理局 (HRSA) 進行。

The HRSA investigation, which concluded last year, examined 351 NFH cases in which organ donations had been authorized but were ultimately canceled. In 103 cases, investigators found “concerning features,” including 73 cases in which patients had neurological signs that should have made them ineligible for organ donation.

HRSA 的調查於去年結束，審查了 351 起已獲授權但最終取消的 NFH 器官捐贈案例。在 103 起案例中，調查人員發現了「令人擔憂的特徵」，其中包括 73 起病人有神經系統徵兆，這本應使他們不符合器官捐贈的資格。

The scrutiny over NFH and other organ procurement organizations comes amid increases in donations from people who have had circulatory deaths (their heart stops), rather than those who have been declared brain-dead. Donation after circulatory death often occurs when patients are not expected to make a recovery and a family decides to withdraw life support. Organ procurement organizations cannot declare a person dead and are barred by law from being involved in care decisions. However, deciding when to remove life support is not clear-cut.

對 NFH 和其他器官採集機構的審查，正值循環系統死亡（心臟停止）者的捐贈增加之際，而非被宣告腦死者的捐贈。循環系統死亡後的捐贈通常發生在病人預期無法康復，且家屬決定撤除生命維持系統時。器官採集機構不能宣告一個人死亡，且法律禁止其參與醫療決策。然而，決定何時撤除生命維持系統並非界限分明。

NFH made headlines when reports surfaced about a 2021 incident in which a Kentucky man in his 30s began visibly crying, pulled his knees to his chest, and shook his head as he was wheeled into surgery to have his organs harvested. The man, Anthony Thomas Hoover II, had overdosed and had been unresponsive for two days before his family agreed to donate his organs, believing he would not recover.

NFH 在有關 2021 年一起事件的報導浮出水面時登上了頭條，當時一名 30 多歲的肯塔基州男子被推入手術室準備摘取器官時，明顯開始哭泣、雙膝抱胸並搖頭。這名男子名為安東尼·托馬斯·胡佛二世 (Anthony Thomas Hoover II)，他在家屬相信他無法康復並同意捐贈器官之前，因服藥過量且已昏迷兩天。

But as the organ retrieval neared, he began showing signs of improvement, which allegedly went ignored by organ procurement workers while making the hospital staff deeply uncomfortable. NFH’s predecessor organization, KODA, allegedly pressured hospital staff to move forward with the organ donation. When Hoover was brought into the operating room clearly responsive, doctors involved in the organ retrieval refused to move forward and remove life support.

但隨著器官摘取手術臨近，他開始顯示出好轉的跡象，據稱這些跡象被器官採集工作人員忽視，同時讓醫院工作人員感到深感不安。NFH 的前身組織 KODA 據稱向醫院工作人員施壓，要求繼續進行器官捐贈。當胡佛被帶入手術室且顯然有反應時，參與器官摘取的醫生拒絕繼續進行並撤除生命維持系統。

One worker, Natasha Miller, told NPR that a KODA supervisor was on the phone at the time, demanding that employees find another surgeon who would agree to carry out the retrieval.

一名工作人員納塔莎·米勒 (Natasha Miller) 告訴 NPR，當時一名 KODA 主管正在通話中，要求員工尋找另一位願意執行摘取手術的醫生。

The surgery was canceled, and Hoover recovered, though he has lingering neurological injuries. His sister appeared at a press briefing Wednesday announcing the US health department’s efforts to decertify NFH.

手術被取消了，胡佛隨後康復，儘管他留下了持久的神經系統損傷。他的姐姐週三出席了一場新聞發布會，宣布美國衛生部門致力於撤銷 NFH 認證的努力。

In a statement, NFH said it complies with all federal organ policies and, since the incident, implemented a “pause” process that allows anyone involved in a case—from family to health care providers—to pause donation plans if concerns arise. The state of Kentucky subsequently wrote the pause process into law.

在一份聲明中，NFH 表示其遵守所有聯邦器官政策，且自該事件以來，實施了一項「暫停」流程，允許參與案件的任何人（從家屬到醫療保健提供者）在出現疑慮時暫停捐贈計劃。肯塔基州隨後將該暫停流程寫入法律。

More than 103,000 people are on the US organ transplant waiting list, and there were more than 49,000 transplants last year.

全美有超過 103,000 人在器官移植等待名單上，去年進行了超過 49,000 例移植手術。

整篇摘要

美國衛生部門正著手關閉一家肯塔基州的器官捐贈機構，主因是該機構涉及嚴重的病人安全疏失，甚至曾試圖從一名仍有生命跡象且有反應的男子身上摘取器官。聯邦調查顯示，該機構在多起案例中無視病人的神經反應，不當推進捐贈流程。儘管該機構計畫上訴，此事件已促使肯塔基州立法增設「暫停」機制，以確保捐贈者的安全與權利。
