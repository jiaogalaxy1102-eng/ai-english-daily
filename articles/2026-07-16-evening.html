David Barnett, co-founder of Noble Signs, joined us on the Nicer Tuesdays stage in New York City to talk about what he loves most: signage, especially in the Big Apple. The graphic designer talked about collecting over 200 storefront signs in New York City and how the studio draws everything by hand, is shaped by reviving vanishing signage and how the team restores loved and lost signs to clients such as bakeries and delis.

Noble Signs 的共同創辦人 David Barnett 加入了我們在紐約市舉辦的 Nicer Tuesdays 講座，分享他最熱愛的事物：招牌，特別是位於大蘋果（紐約）的招牌。這位平面設計師談到了他在紐約市收集了超過 200 個店面招牌的故事，以及他的工作室如何堅持全手工繪製、如何受到復興消逝中招牌的影響，以及團隊如何為麵包店和熟食店等客戶修復那些備受喜愛但已損毀的招牌。
