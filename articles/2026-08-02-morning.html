More confirmation that price hikes and RAM cuts are coming our way.

更多證據顯示，價格上漲和記憶體縮減即將到來。

Android Headlines claims to have the specs and price for the entire Pixel 11 lineup. What the site shared basically lines up with everything else that we’ve heard in the lead-up to the August 12th event. The Pixel 11 is expected to get a $100 price hike, starting at $899, but will come with 256GB of storage instead of 128GB in the base model. While the entire lineup will sport the new Tensor G6 and Titan M3 chips, the base model Pixel 11 Pro and Pixel 11 Pro XL are both getting that rumored RAM cut to just 12GB, along with price increases to $1,099 and $1,299, respectively.

《Android Headlines》宣稱已掌握整個 Pixel 11 系列的規格與價格。該網站分享的資訊基本上與我們在 8 月 12 日發表會前所聽到的一切相符。Pixel 11 預計將調漲 100 美元，起售價為 899 美元，但基礎型號的儲存空間將從 128GB 增加到 256GB。雖然整個系列都將搭載全新的 Tensor G6 和 Titan M3 晶片，但基礎款的 Pixel 11 Pro 和 Pixel 11 Pro XL 記憶體據傳都將縮減至僅 12GB，且價格將分別調漲至 1,099 美元和 1,299 美元。

The Pixel 11 will supposedly ditch the telephoto lens and settle for a dual rear camera setup instead. The pro models appear like they’ll keep the same camera setup from the Pixel 10 lineup. The displays also seem to be largely unchanged from the previous generation. The Pixel 11 Pro Fold wasn’t immune to RAMageddon, either, and will also start with 12GB of RAM and a new price of $1,899.

Pixel 11 據稱將捨棄望遠鏡頭，改用雙後置鏡頭配置。Pro 型號的鏡頭配置似乎將維持與 Pixel 10 系列相同。螢幕顯示方面也與前代產品基本保持不變。Pixel 11 Pro Fold 也無法免於這場「記憶體末日」，同樣將以 128GB 的記憶體起跳，且新售價為 1,899 美元。
