How Maison de Sabré built a cult business of bag charms from scraps

Maison de Sabré has spent nearly a decade building a reputation for colorful, beautifully crafted leather handbags. But lately, the thing that makes people fall for the brand isn’t a bag at all—it’s the charms.

Maison de Sabré 近十年來致力於為其色彩繽紛、工藝精湛的皮革手提包建立名聲。但最近，讓大眾愛上這個品牌的原因根本不是包包，而是吊飾。

Its newest collection features tiny, intricate leather flowers—cherry blossoms, sunflowers, and daisies—that clip onto a strap, adding a jolt of personality to an otherwise formal bag. There’s also a bumblebee and a snail to round out the garden themes. Last season’s collection included jewel-toned fruits and vegetables: snap peas, carrots, chilis, and mushrooms. A recentPikachu capsule,timed toPokémon’s 30th anniversary, sold out almost immediately.

其最新系列以精細的微型皮革花卉為特色——櫻花、向日葵和雛菊——可夾在背帶上，為原本正式的包包增添活潑的個性。還有大黃蜂和蝸牛來使花園主題更完整。上季系列包含寶石色調的水果與蔬菜：甜豌豆、胡蘿蔔、辣椒和蘑菇。近期為慶祝寶可夢 30 週年推出的皮卡丘膠囊系列幾乎立即售罄。

What most customers don’t realize is that those charms are born on the cutting-room floor, from the scraps of colorful leather left from other bags. These offcuts are part of an ongoing “Sabrémoji” charm line that keeps Maison de Sabré customers coming back to the brand season after season. Since they’re limited-edition offerings—dependent on what offcuts are currently available—they’ve also become collectibles. It’s a waste-prevention strategy that generates sales and lets customers personalize a classic bag.

大多數顧客沒意識到，那些吊飾誕生於裁剪室，是由製作其他包包後剩餘的彩色皮革邊角料製成的。這些邊角料是持續推出的「Sabrémoji」吊飾系列的一部分，吸引著 Maison de Sabré 的顧客季復一季地回歸。由於它們是限量版商品——取決於目前現有的邊角料——因此也具備了收藏價值。這是一種防止浪費的策略，既能產生銷售，又讓顧客能個性化經典包款。

The clearest sign of how central those charms are to the brand’s identity is the newest bag in the collection. Maison de Sabré recently launched the Soft Trio, a crossbody deliberately designed with clasps and hooks to show off the charms. It’s part of the company’s broader ambition of building the next generation of luxury brand, where sustainability is a central value.

這些吊飾對品牌識別的重要性，最明顯的跡象是系列中的新款包。Maison de Sabré 最近推出了 Soft Trio，這是一款斜背包，刻意設計了扣環和掛鉤來展示吊飾。這是該公司更大的抱負的一部分，即打造以永續發展為核心價值的下一代奢侈品牌。

The New Rules of Luxury

奢侈品的新規則

Maison de Sabré was founded by brothers Omar and Zane Sabré, who grew up in New Zealand. They originally trained as dentists, but when their father got sick, they realized they didn’t want to spend their lives pursuing careers that weren’t emotionally satisfying to them. As they talked about the future, they discovered a mutual passion for building a luxury brand that would carry their family name and outlive them.

Maison de Sabré 由在紐西蘭長大的 Omar 和 Zane Sabré 兄弟創立。他們最初受訓成為牙醫，但當父親生病時，他們意識到自己不想把一生花在追求無法帶給他們情感滿足感的職業上。當他們談論未來時，他們發現了共同的熱情，那就是建立一個能承載家族姓氏且比他們更長久的奢侈品牌。

They poured $40,000 of savings into their new label, which launched out of Australia. Its very first product, a line of colorful, leather-wrapped iPhone cases, generated $2 million in revenue in its first year. After six years of selling directly to consumers, the company—entirely self-funded—has now expanded into retailers like Nordstrom, Bloomingdale’s, Saks, and Le Bon Marché in Paris. This has accelerated their growth over the past three years. Today, the U.S. is their biggest market.

他們將 4 萬美元的積蓄投入到這個在澳洲啟動的新品牌中。其第一款產品是一系列色彩繽紛的皮革包覆 iPhone 手機殼，在第一年就產生了 200 萬美元的營收。在直接面向消費者銷售六年後，這家完全自籌資金的公司目前已擴展至 Nordstrom、Bloomingdale’s、Saks 和巴黎的 Le Bon Marché 等零售商。這在過去三年中加速了他們的成長。如今，美國是他們最大的市場。

The Sabré brothers have spent a lot of time thinking about what a modern luxury brand should be. Like older generations of luxury consumers, millennials and Gen Zers prize beauty, good design, and durability, but they also expect luxury to be joyful, sustainable, and fairly priced. (Maison de Sabré bags cost $400 to $800, which is between a third and a quarter of the price of brands like Louis Vuitton and Chanel, even though they use similar-quality leather.)

Sabré 兄弟花了大量時間思考現代奢侈品牌應該是什麼樣子。與老一輩的奢侈品消費者一樣，千禧世代和 Z 世代重視美感、優良設計和耐用性，但他們也期望奢侈品是令人愉悅、永續且價格合理的。（Maison de Sabré 的包款售價為 400 至 800 美元，約為路易威登和香奈兒等品牌價格的三分之一到四分之一，儘管它們使用的是品質相近的皮革。）
