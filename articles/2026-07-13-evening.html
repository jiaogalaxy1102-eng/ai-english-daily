We chat to the creator and co-writer / director of the show about their love for amateur filmmaking, iconic TV references, and the fun they had making that unnerving Yooglet.

我們訪談了這部劇集的創作者與共同編劇／導演，探討他們對業餘電影製作的熱愛、經典的電視參考資料，以及在製作那個令人不安的 Yooglet 時所獲得的樂趣。

Sam Campbell and Joe Pelling are both masters of deadpan silliness – Sam in his hugely successful comedy career, and Joe in co-creating Don’t Hug Me I’m Scared. The two have worked together in the past, but Make That Movie (produced by Blink Industries, now a hit show on Channel 4) is their first creation as a duo. Dreamt up as a parody of cringey, chaotic makeover shows like Changing Rooms with the unhinged humour of The Mighty Boosh, the mockumentary sees hotshot director Sam Campbell and his team find “everyday” people who have an idea for a film, and attempt to make their movie into a reality.

Sam Campbell 和 Joe Pelling 都是冷面幽默（deadpan silliness）的大師——Sam 在他大獲成功的喜劇生涯中展現了這一點，而 Joe 則共同創作了《別抱我，我會怕》（Don’t Hug Me I’m Scared）。兩人過去曾合作過，但《Make That Movie》（由 Blink Industries 製作，現為 Channel 4 的熱門節目）是他們作為拍檔的第一部作品。這部偽紀錄片被構思為對《裝修大改造》（Changing Rooms）等尷尬且混亂的改造節目的惡搞，並融合了《魔幻之旅》（The Mighty Boosh）那種瘋癲的幽默，劇情講述當紅導演 Sam Campbell 及其團隊尋找有電影想法的「平凡」人，並嘗試將他們的電影夢想變為現實。

This concept presented Sam and Joe with the perfect vessel to incorporate all sorts of styles of animation, bringing to life their clients’ ridiculous ideas. There’s CGI snakes, a 3D virtual girlfriend named Super Breast, a hand-drawn animation about the barefoot community, a sci-fi epic inside a retro internet landscape, and, of course, the creepy-cute Yooglet. These sequences turn this surreal, very funny comedy show into something totally unique, and really showcases the duo’s original approach to filmmaking. Here, Sam and Joe shed some light on their partnership, process, influences, decisions, and dreams for the future of MTM…

這個概念為 Sam 和 Joe 提供了一個完美的媒介，可以融入各式各樣的動畫風格，將客戶那些荒謬的想法變為現實。片中有 CGI 蛇、一個名叫 Super Breast 的 3D 虛擬女友、一部關於赤腳社群的手繪動畫、一段發生在復古網路景觀中的科幻史詩，當然還有既詭異又可愛的 Yooglet。這些片段將這部超現實且非常有趣的喜劇節目轉化為某種完全獨特的作品，並真正展示了這對拍檔獨創的電影製作方式。在這裡，Sam 和 Joe 為我們揭示了他們的合作關係、製作過程、影響來源、決策以及對《MTM》未來的夢想……

Joe Pelling and Sam Campbell: Make That Movie, The Rescue Most Brave

Joe Pelling 和 Sam Campbell：《Make That Movie》，最勇敢的救援

It’s Nice That: First off, can you tell us about your background working together, and how partnering on Make That Movie came about? What led to you wanting to make this type of show?

It’s Nice That：首先，能請兩位聊聊合作的背景，以及合作《Make That Movie》的契機嗎？是什麼讓你們想製作這種類型的節目？

Joe Pelling: I met Sam after seeing his Edinburgh show in 2017. I think our mutual friend Charlie Perkins introduced us when she was working as head of comedy at Blink Industries. We made a short Comedy Blap together in 2019 called Get Real Dude, and then Sam worked with us on the TV adaptation of Don’t Hug Me I’m Scared for Channel 4. Sam has a very original voice and doesn’t massively overthink stuff, so we always found it pretty enjoyable working with him on that show and he seemed to get the tone we were going for. I think the idea for Make That Movie came from Sam’s love of terrible amateur filmmaking and a lot of the chaos that goes down on set. Documentaries like Lost Soul: The Doomed Journey of Richard Stanley’s Island of Dr. Moreau about Richard Stanley’s nightmare making a film with Marlon Brando were a big influence.

Joe Pelling：我在 2017 年看了 Sam 在愛丁堡的演出後認識了他。我想是我們的共同朋友 Charlie Perkins 在 Blink Industries 擔任喜劇主管時介紹我們認識的。我們在 2019 年合作製作了一部名為《Get Real Dude》的短片《Comedy Blap》，接著 Sam 又與我們一起為 Channel 4 製作了《別抱我，我會怕》的電視改編版。Sam 擁有非常獨特的風格，而且不會過度思考，所以我們一直覺得在那部劇中與他合作非常愉快，他似乎能精準掌握我們想要的基調。我想《Make That Movie》的靈感來自於 Sam 對糟糕的業餘電影製作以及片場各種混亂情況的熱愛。像《迷失靈魂：李察史丹利之魔幻島》（關於李察史丹利與馬龍白蘭度拍片時的噩夢過程）這類紀錄片產生了很大的影響。

I think we wanted to make a comedy show that also had a strong visual flavour, like Garth Marenghi’s Darkplace, Documentary Now! or The Mighty Boosh. This idea meant we could weave that stuff in naturally as part of the overall concept.

我覺得我們想製作一部具有強烈視覺風格的喜劇節目，就像《加斯·馬倫基的黑暗地點》（Garth Marenghi’s Darkplace）、《紀錄片進行時》（Documentary Now!）或《魔幻之旅》。這個點子意味著我們可以將這些元素自然地編織進整體概念中。

Sam Campbell: My cousin showed me the original Don’t Hug Me I’m Scared short on Youtube back in the day. My memory is hazy but I remember looking up the creators and saw they were in an art collective. I pictured them all living in a commune and waking up at the crack of dawn to build puppets and film maggots.

Sam Campbell：我表弟早些年在 YouTube 上給我看了原版的《別抱我，我會怕》短片。我的記憶很模糊，但我記得當時查了創作者的資料，發現他們在一個藝術公社裡。我幻想他們都住在公社裡，黎明時分就起床製作木偶和拍攝蛆蟲。

Sorry to nitpick but I actually met Joe in 2018 not 2017. He really does lie through his teeth! I found him very funny to talk to and indeed look at (pencil neck) so when the offer came through to make a Blap with him and evil production company Blink it felt like a no-brainer.

抱歉我要挑剔一下，我其實是在 2018 年而不是 2017 年認識 Joe 的。他真的是在滿口胡言！我覺得跟他聊天非常有趣，看著他也很有趣（鉛筆般的脖子），所以當收到與他和「邪惡」的製作公司 Blink 合作製作 Blap 的邀約時，這簡直是不假思索的決定。

Will Kay, who was working on an animated sequence for Dora the Explorer movie at the time and ended up producing Make That Movie, was tasked with making the dream helmet because he has an affinity with wires and cords and the like.

當時正在為《愛探險的朵拉》電影製作動畫片段的 Will Kay 後來成為了《Make That Movie》的製作人，他被委派製作「夢想頭盔」，因為他對電線、繩索之類的東西情有獨鍾。

通往《Make That Movie》的道路之崎嶇，即使是經驗最豐富的旅人也會感到極度暈車。我相信這個點子的萌芽來自我們 2019 年的初步會議。這段評論來自一份記錄雜亂概念的 Excel 表格：

「我們找一些冷門的電子書，訪問作者並改編他們作品中的一個場景。——這不是很酷嗎？」

Joe Pelling and Sam Campbell: Make That Movie

Joe Pelling 和 Sam Campbell：《Make That Movie》

“I think the idea for Make That Movie came from Sam’s love of terrible amateur filmmaking and a lot of the chaos that goes down on set.” Joe Pelling
「我想《Make That Movie》的靈感來自於 Sam 對糟糕的業餘電影製作以及片場各種混亂情況的熱愛。」Joe Pelling

INT:The show is so funny and original, I loved it, and you’ve had some great press, congrats! I want to dig more into the visual approaches you took to create the world of the show and for comedic effect. So, overall, what did you want the show to look and feel like?

INT：這部劇非常幽默且具原創性，我很喜歡，而且你們獲得了很好的媒體評價，恭喜！我想進一步探討你們為了創造劇中世界以及喜劇效果所採取的視覺手法。總體來說，你們希望這部劇看起來和感覺起來是什麼樣子？

JP: Thanks for saying that! We knew we wanted the film sections of the show to be different each time, and not necessarily super low budget looking, just poorly conceived with idiotic high concept premises. So the rest of the episodes needed to feel more naturalistic and stripped back, hence why we shot handheld and quite scrappy for the ‘real’ sections and then often switched lenses and lighting styles for the movies. Part of this was just about being able to shoot quickly as we had lots to get through each day in all these different locations across the series.

JP：謝謝你這麼說！我們知道我們希望劇中的電影部分每次都有所不同，且不一定要看起來預算極低，只需看起來構思粗劣、帶有愚蠢的高概念前提即可。因此，劇集的其餘部分需要感覺更自然且簡約，這就是為什麼我們在「現實」部分採用手持拍攝且較為隨性的風格，然後在電影部分則經常切換鏡頭和燈光風格。部分原因也是為了能夠快速拍攝，因為我們每天在系列中的各個地點都有大量的內容要完成。

SC: The feel! I wish the show was released alongside a set of long lumpy tactile shapes corresponding with each episode so people could actually feel the show.

SC：那種感覺！我希望這部劇發行時能附帶一組與每一集對應的長條狀、凹凸不平的觸覺形狀，這樣人們就能真正「感覺」到這部劇。

As for the look, every department had a lot of input. Except for the sound department of course, they are only really interested in audio and for some reason plane-spotting. It is not a serious show so we tried to squeeze in a lot of wild styles and usually went for big swings over subtle nods. I often completely miss subtle nods and have to have them explained to me by a staff member at the clinic.

至於外觀，每個部門都有很多投入。當然除了音效部門，他們真的只對音頻感興趣，而且出於某種原因還喜歡賞機（plane-spotting）。這不是一部嚴肅的劇，所以我們嘗試塞進很多狂野的風格，通常選擇大膽的嘗試而非微妙的暗示。我經常完全忽略微妙的暗示，還得讓診所的工作人員向我解釋。

INT: What other shows or genres were you looking to for inspiration, or to parody?

INT：你們還參考了哪些其他節目或類型來獲取靈感或進行惡搞？

JP: With the sci-fi stuff in episode one, we looked at a lot of bad naughties films where everyone had extra complicated facial accessories and leather. Films like Swordfish and In Time were definitely on the moodboard. Then with the teen love story and the bog man we wanted it to feel more bright and 80/90s, like the film Heathers. Our costume designer Ailsa Windsor did an amazing job at pulling the right references for these sections.

JP：在第一集的科幻內容中，我們參考了許多千禧年代（naughties）的爛片，那裡的每個人都有額外複雜的面部飾品和皮革。像《劍魚》（Swordfish）和《鐘點戰》（In Time）肯定都在情緒板上。至於青少年愛情故事和沼澤人，我們希望它感覺更明亮，有 80/90 年代的感覺，就像電影《希德姐妹幫》（Heathers）。我們的服裝設計師 Ailsa Windsor 在為這些部分挑選合適的參考資料方面做得非常出色。

SC: I wasn’t on the hunt for visual references during the writing so much. For a while I was trying to study episodes of Arrested Development to see how they manage to juggle so many storylines, which looking back was the errand of a certain fool.

SC：在寫作期間，我並沒有刻意尋找視覺參考。有一段時間，我試圖研究《發展受阻》（Arrested Development）的劇集，看看他們如何處理這麼多故事線，回想起來，那真是白費力氣。

I would say a bigger inspiration was watching movies like Boondock Saints and The Butterfly Effect at sleepovers as a child.

我會說更大的靈感是小時候在過夜派對上看的電影，比如《處刑人》（Boondock Saints）和《蝴蝶效應》（The Butterfly Effect）。

A decade ago I was living in Sydney and met a fellow called Craig Anderson with a huge VHS collection and he would dig out flicks like Warriors of Virtue – a high fantasy made by some Chinese brothers who were doctors by trade and a Norwegian movie called Body Troopers about a boy who shrinks down and goes inside his grandfather's body to chase out his kidney stones.

十年前我住在悉尼，遇到了一個叫 Craig Anderson 的傢伙，他收藏了大量的 VHS 錄影帶，他會挖出像《五行戰士》（Warriors of Virtue）之類的片子——這是一部由幾位職業是醫生的中國兄弟製作的高奇幻片；還有一部名為《身體特工隊》（Body Troopers）的挪威電影，講述一個男孩縮小後進入祖父體內追逐腎結石的故事。

Joe Pelling and Sam Campbell: Make That Movie, Synthezoidian Elders

Joe Pelling 和 Sam Campbell：《Make That Movie》，合成類人長老

“It is not a serious show so we tried to squeeze in a lot of wild styles and usually went for big swings over subtle nods.” Sam Campbell
「這不是一部嚴肅的劇，所以我們嘗試塞進很多狂野的風格，通常選擇大膽的嘗試而非微妙的暗示。」Sam Campbell

INT: The opening sequence is really fun – what were your references and inspiration here, and how did this come to life through the details?

INT：開場片段真的很有趣——你們在這裡的參考和靈感是什麼？這些細節是如何實現的？

JP: We chatted a lot about the team and their overall look. The idea I suppose is that they see themselves as an elite squadron of filmmakers, so a lot of detail went into their purple outfits and the colour scheme or their HQ. There is an era of light makeover TV shows like Changing Rooms, and Garden Force where the titles have a lot of soft fades, over-the-top graphics and strange music we were trying to emulate. I ended up making the music for the titles and trying to give it a naff world-music feel, after Sam shared some Australian TV shows he liked like Ocean Girl.

JP：我們討論了很多關於團隊及其整體外觀。我設想的想法是，他們自視為電影製作的精英小隊，因此在他們的紫色制服和總部的色調上下了很多工夫。在輕改編電視節目（如《裝修大改造》和《花園武力》）的時代，片頭有很多柔和的淡出、誇張的圖形和奇怪的音樂，我們試圖模仿這些。在 Sam 分享了一些他喜歡的澳洲電視節目（如《海洋女孩》）後，我最終為片頭製作了音樂，試圖給它一種俗氣的世界音樂感。

Challenge Anneka was also a big influence in terms of the show’s overall tone and premise, plus she wears a cool jumpsuit. I’m not sure if they do this kind of thing anymore on TV shows but I think it would be nice if all the dragons on Dragons Den had dragon themed costumes instead of quite expensive business suits.

《挑戰安妮卡》（Challenge Anneka）在節目的整體基調和前提方面也有很大影響，再加上她穿著酷炫的連身褲。我不確定現在的電視節目是否還這麼做，但我認為如果《龍穴》（Dragons Den）中所有的龍（投資人）都穿著龍的主題服裝，而不是昂貴的商務西裝，那會很不錯。

SC: Originally the title sequence was more directly aping a sort of A-Team or Mission Impossible vibe which you’d see DIY and gardening shows in Australia doing in the early 2000’s and always felt pretty OTT given the subject matter. Ultimately it felt a bit played out and we tried to go with a more light-hearted vibe. Joe composed the music using his laptop. It is amazing what laptops are capable of with the right keyboard touchings.

SC：最初的片頭序幕更直接地模仿了《天龍特攻隊》（A-Team）或《不可能的任務》（Mission Impossible）的風格，這是澳洲 2000 年代初期的 DIY 和園藝節目常做的，考慮到主題，這總讓人覺得非常誇張。最終我們覺得這有點老套，於是嘗試採用更輕鬆的基調。Joe 用他的筆記型電腦創作了音樂。令人驚訝的是，只要有正確的鍵盤敲擊，筆記型電腦竟然能做這麼多事。

I really do wish every title sequence had different clips and gags for every episode but that is a luxury of time and money that our clock and wallet did not permit.

我真的希望每個片頭序幕在每一集都有不同的剪輯和笑料，但那是我們的時間和財力所不允許的奢望。

Joe Pelling and Sam Campbell: Make That Movie, opening sequence

Joe Pelling 和 Sam Campbell：《Make That Movie》，開場片段

INT: There’s lots of different types and styles of animation in the show. Why did you take this approach? What did you want animation to add to the story and the comedy?

INT：劇中有很多不同類型和風格的動畫。為什麼採取這種手法？你們希望動畫為故事和喜劇效果增添什麼？

JP: We both like animation, Sam also studied animation before doing stand-up and we probably wasted a lot of the writing time we had drawing horrible little characters. I guess we wanted the show to have a bit of that mixed-media feel, where there is a lot crammed into 22 mins.

JP：我們兩人都喜歡動畫，Sam 在做單口喜劇之前也學過動畫，我們可能把很多寫作時間浪費在了畫恐怖的小角色上。我想我們希望這部劇有一種多媒體融合的感覺，在 22 分鐘內塞進很多東西。

With the Yooglet episode the idea was that this stupid-looking woodland folk creature was a national treasure with a long history like Paddington or Winnie the Pooh. So we tried to show him in a variety of styles on book covers that stretched back to the 1940s and then more garish modern versions. We also looked at a lot of old books that famously have not aged well, like the Tin Tin stuff.

在《Yooglet》那一集中，想法是這個看起來很蠢的林地生物是一個有著悠久歷史的國寶，就像《柏靈頓熊》或《小熊維尼》。所以我們試圖在回溯到 1940 年代的書封上以各種風格展示他，然後是更花哨的現代版本。我們還參考了許多公認隨時代流逝而顯得不合時宜的老書，比如《丁丁歷險記》之類的東西。

There is also the 2D-animated section in the last episode that was essentially a parody of outlandish 1970/80s films like René Laloux’s Gandahar and maybe a bit of The Land Before Time. I love all that stuff.

最後一集還有一段 2D 動畫部分，基本上是對 1970/80 年代奇特電影的惡搞，如 René Laloux 的《甘達星》（Gandahar），可能還有點《歷險小恐龍》（The Land Before Time）的影子。我熱愛那些東西。

SC: We had to find a balance with the show functioning as a sitcom while still trying to include the occasional motion graphic spurts you’d likely see more in a magazine-style show. The Yooglet show was a horrible horrible nightmare for the longest time because it needed a load of these packages built, but it seems to be the one people have responded to the most, based on the text messages I’ve received.

SC：我們必須在節目作為情景喜劇的功能與偶爾加入你在雜誌類節目中看到的動態圖形噴發之間找到平衡。《Yooglet》那集在很長一段時間內都是一場可怕的噩夢，因為它需要建立大量這些組件，但根據我收到的簡訊，它似乎是人們反應最熱烈的一集。

I do feel like we have a lot left to explore. If we were allowed to do more episodes, the crew attempting a bombastic Henry Sellick stop motion film would be fun.

我確實覺得我們還有很多東西可以探索。如果允許我們製作更多集數，讓團隊嘗試製作一部誇張的 Henry Sellick 式定格動畫電影會很有趣。

Also I had the great privilege of performing the motion capture for the 3D character Super Breast in the second episode.

此外，我有幸在第二集中為 3D 角色 Super Breast 進行了動態捕捉表演。

INT: What visual aspects of the show would you want to push further in a second series?

INT：在第二季中，你們希望在節目的哪些視覺層面進一步推動？

JP: Most of the time we were just trying to make sure the story and characters were funny, and the heightened visual stuff was actually something we could afford to do in the time we had.

JP：大部分時間我們只是在努力確保故事和角色有趣，而那些強化的視覺效果其實是我們在有限時間內負擔得起的事情。

There was an MTV-style sequence where we shot on digital, MiniDV and 16mm at the same time. For the mythical bog scene we built the entire thing inside of a school gym and filled it with dry ice and a water tank to make it feel a bit more fake and stylised. We also installed a functioning car turntable in a hotel carpark, because none of the greedy millionaires who have them in their driveways would let us film on them. There were quite a few moments like this where we got to push the boat out and it’s always a bit stressful, while being very satisfying if you can pull it off. Although I did think our producer Will Kay was going to murder me with his brain. Hopefully we will do a series two, where the characters have to make a stop-motion strata-cut animation while they are skydiving.

有一段 MTV 風格的片段，我們同時使用數位、MiniDV 和 16mm 膠卷拍攝。至於神話般的沼澤場景，我們在學校體育館內搭建了整個場景，並填滿了乾冰和水箱，讓它感覺更假、更有風格感。我們還在飯店停車場安裝了一個可運作的汽車轉盤，因為那些在車道上擁有轉盤的貪婪百萬富翁都不讓我們在那裡拍攝。有很多這樣的時刻，我們得以放手一搏，這總是讓人有些壓力，但如果能成功，就會非常令人滿足。儘管我當時覺得我們的製作人 Will Kay 快要用腦波殺了我了。希望我們會做第二季，到時候角色們必須在跳傘時製作一段定格分層切割（strata-cut）動畫。

SC: It is always exciting to try nutty set pieces and in the outline phase we were guilty of chasing those, then sheepishly returning to focusing on story crap and how your characters help or hinder each other, which is the one true key. I have a handful of ideas if the commisi-genie’s grant us the wish of series two. An episode about triplets who have noticed that there are a tonne of movies with twins but their kind rarely sees representation. I haven’t seen this new movie about Anthony Bourdain yet but I would like to make a gritty drama set in a revolving restaurant. Anyway, thanks for interviewing us!

SC：嘗試瘋狂的大場面總是令人興奮，在草擬階段我們常沉迷於追求這些，然後又羞愧地回到關注故事本身，以及角色如何互相幫助或阻礙，這才是唯一的關鍵。如果「委任精靈」能滿足我們製作第二季的願望，我手頭上有一些想法。比如有一集是關於三胞胎的，他們注意到有很多關於雙胞胎的電影，但他們這種人卻很少得到呈現。我還沒看過關於 Anthony Bourdain 的新電影，但我很想製作一部設定在旋轉餐廳的現實主義硬派戲劇。總之，謝謝採訪我們！
