If you’re a LinkedIn user, you recognize it instinctively: Anyone with a green ribbon around their profile picture is in the market for a new job, and they want everyone to know it.

如果你是 LinkedIn 用戶，你會直覺地認出它：頭像周圍帶有綠色緞帶的任何人都在尋找新工作，且他們希望讓每個人都知道。

LinkedIn’s “Open to Work” badge, which has now been part of the platform for six years, has become a distinct visual shorthand for frequent posters on the platform. Recently, however, its merits have come under fire.

LinkedIn 的「Open to Work」徽章已在該平台推出六年，已成為該平台頻繁發文者的獨特視覺代號。然而，最近它的優點受到了批評。

On July 19, Ben Lang, an engineer at the AI coding platform Cursor AI, took to X with a message for his followers. “Tip: never use the green Open to Work badge on LinkedIn,” the post reads. “Negative signal for hiring managers and recruiters.”

7 月 19 日，AI 程式碼平台 Cursor AI 的工程師 Ben Lang 在 X 上向他的追隨者發布了一則訊息。貼文寫道：「建議：永遠不要在 LinkedIn 上使用綠色的 Open to Work 徽章，這對招募經理和招聘人員來說是負面信號。」

The post has since amassed more than a million views and hundreds of comments, and notched a featured post under X’s trending topics.

此後，該貼文累積了超過一百萬次的瀏覽量和數百條評論，並登上了 X 熱門話題下的精選貼文。

This isn’t the first time LinkedIn’s Open to Work badge has attracted debate. In fact, the discourse has been ongoing for years, with commenters generally falling into two camps: One side argues the design of the badge gives off an “air of desperation” to recruiters; the other views the badge as a crucial tool for grabbing recruiters’ attention on the crowded platform.

這不是 LinkedIn 的 Open to Work 徽章第一次引發爭論。事實上，這場討論已經持續多年，評論者通常分為兩派：一方認為徽章的設計向招聘人員散發出一種「絕望的氣息」；另一方則認為該徽章是在擁擠的平台上吸引招聘人員注意的關鍵工具。

According to LinkedIn, more than 60 million users currently have the public #OpenToWork frame on their profile pictures. Those members, it says, are nearly two times as likely to get a message from a recruiter than those who don’t. They’re also finding jobs more than 20% faster than job seekers who don’t display the Open to Work badge.

根據 LinkedIn 的數據，目前有超過 6,000 萬用戶在其個人資料頭像上使用了公開的 #OpenToWork 框。該公司表示，這些成員收到招聘人員訊息的可能性是未使用者的近兩倍。他們找到工作的速度也比不顯示 Open to Work 徽章的求職者快 20% 以上。

LinkedIn’s data appears to show that the Open to Work profile badge does its job—but its enduring negative reputation signals that it might still be time for a redesign.

LinkedIn 的數據似乎顯示 Open to Work 個人資料徽章發揮了作用，但它揮之不去的負面名聲顯示，現在可能仍是重新設計的時候了。
