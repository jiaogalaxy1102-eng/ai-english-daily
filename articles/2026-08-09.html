In July, I sat among roughly 100 people in a packed conference room in Taipei as a speaker highlighted the industrial parks and tax rates of an American city nearly 7,000 miles away.

七月時，我坐在台北一間擠滿人的會議室裡，大約有一百人聽著講者介紹一個近七千英里外的美國城市，其工業園區與稅率。

“There are ribbon-cutting ceremonies almost every week. Everywhere you look, something is being built. This trend will continue for the next 20 to 30 years.”

「幾乎每週都有剪綵儀式。放眼望去，到處都在建設。這個趨勢將持續未來二十到三十年。」

Steve Hsu, head of the Arizona Commerce Authority’s Taiwan office, was pitching real estate opportunities in Phoenix — the desert city that is undergoing a huge economic transformation known as the “TSMC effect.”

亞利桑那州商務廳（Arizona Commerce Authority）台灣辦事處處長徐竹先（Steve Hsu）正在推銷鳳凰城的房地產機會——這座沙漠城市正經歷被稱為「台積電效應」的巨大經濟轉型。

Taiwan Semiconductor Manufacturing Company, the world’s leading chipmaker, had announced its plans to construct fabrication plants in Arizona in 2020. Today, the expanding TSMC project in the state represents the largest single foreign investment project in U.S. history.

全球領先的晶片製造商台積電（TSMC）於 2020 年宣布在亞利桑那州建造晶圓廠的計畫。如今，該公司在該州持續擴張的計畫代表了美國史上最大規模的單一外國投資案。

As Arizona’s economic fortunes have become increasingly tied to TSMC and its chip supply chain, it’s been keen to diversify the roster of investors beyond tech companies.

隨著亞利桑那州的經濟命運與台積電及其晶片供應鏈的聯繫日益緊密，該州一直渴望將投資者名單擴展到科技公司之外。

At the event, Hsu urged Taiwanese property developers and family offices to invest in the support infrastructure — warehouses, logistics centers, hotels, commercial buildings, and science parks — needed for the growing network of suppliers following TSMC to the desert.

在活動中，徐竹先敦促台灣房地產開發商和家族辦公室投資支撐性基礎建設——包括倉庫、物流中心、飯店、商業大樓和科學園區——這些是跟隨台積電來到沙漠的日益壯大的供應商網絡所需要的。

“The investment opportunity in Arizona is not just available for tech companies — it’s an opportunity for everyone, including investors in hospitality, family offices, and other industries,” Hsu told the room. “If only tech companies are building there while we ignore the appreciation in land values, we’ll be missing a once-in-a-lifetime opportunity.”

「亞利桑那州的投資機會不只開放給科技公司——這對每個人來說都是機會，包括觀光餐飲業、家族辦公室和其他產業的投資者，」徐竹先對全場說。「如果只有科技公司在那裡建設，而我們忽視了土地價值的增值，我們將錯過一個一生一次的機會。」

Driven by tariffs and U.S. efforts to reshore semiconductor manufacturing, TSMC has steadily expanded its Arizona plans. Earlier in July, the company announced an additional $100 billion investment, bringing its planned U.S. spending to $265 billion across 12 facilities. At least 25 Taiwanese suppliers have established operations in the U.S. as they follow their biggest customer overseas, according to Taiwanese newspaper Economic Daily News.

在關稅和美國重振半導體製造努力的推動下，台積電穩步擴大其亞利桑那州的計畫。七月初，該公司宣布追加 1,000 億美元投資，使其在美國的計畫支出達到 2,650 億美元，涵蓋 12 座設施。根據《經濟日報》報導，至少有 25 家台灣供應商跟隨其最大的客戶到海外，在美國建立了據點。

Arizona’s increasing focus on Taiwan comes as trade between the two jumped from $4.9 billion in 2024 to $21.2 billion in 2025. Last year, Taiwan bypassed Canada and China to become Arizona’s second-largest trading partner in terms of volume after Mexico, but top partner in terms of trading value.

亞利桑那州對台灣的日益重視，正值雙方貿易額從 2024 年的 49 億美元跳升至 2025 年的 212 億美元。去年，台灣超越加拿大和中國，成為亞利桑那州交易量第二大的貿易夥伴，僅次於墨西哥，但在交易價值方面則是首位夥伴。

Tapping into the U.S. market offers huge potential, but international investors still have to consider a tricky web of taxes, regulations, and red tape. During the coffee break at the conference, I wandered around the room to ask attendees if they were actually planning to invest.

進入美國市場具有巨大的潛力，但國際投資者仍必須考慮複雜的稅務、法規和繁文縟節。在會議的茶歇時間，我在會場巡視，詢問參與者是否真的計畫投資。

Several venture capital and family office representatives seemed excited and told me they plan to join investment delegation trips to Arizona soon.

幾位風險投資和家族辦公室的代表似乎很興奮，並告訴我他們計畫近期參加赴亞利桑那州的投資考察團。

For some other attendees, the future of Arizona was much more personal. The chief financial officer of a Taiwanese real estate company told me her firm was probably too small to invest on that scale.

對於其他一些參與者來說，亞利桑那州的未來更具個人色彩。一家台灣房地產公司的財務長告訴我，她的公司規模可能太小，無法進行那種規模的投資。

“But my son studies in Arizona now,” she said. “I want to understand what the future for a Taiwanese person might look like over there if he decides to stay.”

「但我兒子現在在亞利桑那州讀書，」她說。「我想了解如果他決定留在那裡，對一個台灣人來說，那裡的未來會是什麼樣子。」

整篇摘要

亞利桑那州正因台積電的大規模投資而經歷經濟轉型，當地官員正積極引導台灣投資者將目光投向晶片產業之外。除了半導體供應鏈，亞利桑那州也希望吸引基礎建設、房地產及觀光服務業的資金。儘管潛在商機龐大，國際投資者仍需面對稅務與法規等複雜挑戰。
