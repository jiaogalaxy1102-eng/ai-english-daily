The ignored, ugly utilities that run your home just got a major redesign

Could you love your furnace? Would you put your water heater on display? Is your air conditioner a reflection of your personal design aesthetic?

你會愛上你的暖爐嗎？你會把熱水器擺出來展示嗎？你的冷氣機是否反映了你的個人設計美學？

Unless you are very, very weird, the answer to all those questions is no. A new home utilities startup called Everyday^ wants to make you say yes.

除非你非常、非常古怪，否則對這些問題的回答都是「不」。一家名為 Everyday^ 的新居家設施新創公司想要讓你說「是」。

Everyday^ has just launched what it calls an integrated home infrastructure system made up of the seemingly boring utilities of the home: HVAC systems, water heaters, air purifiers, and thermostats. A single-unit Air^ system will sell for about $2,280 and first be available in the European market.

Everyday^ 剛推出了一套所謂的整合式居家基礎設施系統，由看似乏味的居家設施組成：暖通空調（HVAC）系統、熱水器、空氣清淨機和恆溫器。一套單機版的 Air^ 系統售價約為 2,280 美元，並將首先在歐洲市場上市。

Home utilities are “something that people rarely think about,” says cofounder Morten Meisner-Jensen. “But they’re actually where the most money is spent and they’re some of the systems that we depend [on] the most. And some of these devices have some of the largest impacts on the global climate.”

共同創辦人 Morten Meisner-Jensen 表示，居家設施是「人們很少思考的東西」。「但它們實際上是花費最多資金的地方，也是我們最依賴的系統之一。而且其中一些設備對全球氣候有著巨大的影響。」

Everyday^, based in Copenhagen, has reconsidered these utilities through the lens of design and artificial intelligence, developing customizable devices that mesh with the furnishings in modern homes while also using AI to optimize how they perform.

總部位於哥本哈根的 Everyday^ 透過設計與人工智慧的視角重新審視了這些設施，開發出可客製化的設備，能與現代家居擺設融合，同時利用人工智慧優化其效能。

The first product, which was announced earlier this summer, is called Air^. It’s a heat-pump-connected heating, cooling, and air purification unit. Its minimalist design and profile can be customized with interchangeable material surfaces. The company plans to expand its product line to include hot water, energy storage, and broader home energy systems.

第一款產品於今年夏天稍早發布，名為 Air^。這是一款連接熱泵的供暖、製冷和空氣淨化單元。其極簡主義的設計與外型可以透過更換材質表面進行客製化。該公司計劃擴展其產品線，納入熱水、儲能和更廣泛的家庭能源系統。

Meisner-Jensen, who previously founded the stand-alone office pod maker Room, says the idea for Everyday^ came from noticing just how ugly, overlooked, and complicated most home utilities are.

Meisner-Jensen 曾創立獨立辦公艙製造商 Room，他表示 Everyday^ 的構思源於注意到大多數居家設施是多麼醜陋、被忽視且複雜。
