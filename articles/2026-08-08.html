Volkswagen plans to win America back with a pickup, report says

A US-made pickup truck by 2030 is part of Volkswagen Group’s plan to revive its American business. According to a Reuters report, entering this competitive but high-margin market, as well as the large SUV segment, is the path forward, since both are areas the automaker currently ignores.

大眾集團計畫在 2030 年前推出一款美國製造的皮卡貨車，作為振興其美國業務計畫的一部分。根據路透社報導，進入這個競爭激烈但利潤豐厚的市場，以及大型 SUV 領域，是前進的道路，因為這兩個領域是該汽車製造商目前所忽視的。

The report is anonymously sourced, and VW declined to comment, so take the news with a pinch of salt. But it does make sense: Love it or hate it, the four-door pickup truck is the go-to for Americans who want one vehicle that can carry passengers and cargo that doesn’t need to be shielded from the elements. You might think a hatchback or a station wagon would be just as good or better, but that’s probably why you’re reading Ars Technica in the first place.

該報導來源匿名，且大眾汽車拒絕置評，因此對此消息應持保留態度。但這確實合乎邏輯：不論喜不喜歡，四門皮卡是那些想要一輛既能載客又能載貨，且貨物不需要遮風擋雨的美國人的首選。你可能認為掀背車或旅行車同樣好甚至更好，但這可能就是你當初會閱讀 Ars Technica 的原因。

Tariffs and an antediluvian approach to emissions controls might make the US market a haven for vehicles that are uncompetitive or unsellable elsewhere, and the pickup truck market in the US will account for almost $200 billion in sales in 2026, with projections to continue growing each year, so one can certainly see merit in the idea. The global car might be dead, but that just means you need to develop cars for each region’s taste. And Americans really like pickup trucks.

關稅和陳舊的排放控制方法可能使美國市場成為那些在其他地方不具競爭力或無法銷售的車輛的避風港，而美國皮卡市場在 2026 年的銷售額將達到近 2000 億美元，預計每年還會持續增長，因此確實可以看到這個構想的價值。全球化車型可能已經消亡，但這僅意味著你需要根據每個地區的口味來開發汽車。而美國人真的很喜歡皮卡。

And getting its American operations back on track is important for VW Group. Last year was bad for the company’s bottom line, and 2026 looks to be more of the same, as sales fall in China and North America. VW Group executives want to streamline the company by closing factories and laying off workers by 2030 to make it more competitive, and the plan is supported by the Porsche family, which owns just under 32 percent of the company. But the closures and layoffs are vehemently opposed by the state of Lower Saxony, which owns 20 percent of VW Group, as well as the unions, which hold half of the seats on VW Group’s supervisory board.

讓美國業務重回正軌對大眾集團至關重要。去年對公司的淨利潤來說很糟糕，2026 年看起來也會大同小異，因為在中國和北美的銷量正在下降。大眾集團高層希望在 2030 年前透過關閉工廠和裁員來精簡公司，使其更具競爭力，該計畫得到了擁有該公司不到 32% 股份的保時捷家族的支持。但關閉和裁員遭到了擁有大眾集團 20% 股份的下薩克森州以及在集團監事會中佔據半數席位的工會的強烈反對。

Based on Reuters’ report, nothing is finalized, so there are no concrete details at this point. We have no idea what size pickup VW is considering, how much it might cost, or even whether it will be gas- or electric-powered. A few possibilities suggest themselves, though.

根據路透社的報導，目前尚未定案，因此還沒有具體的細節。我們不知道大眾汽車正在考慮什麼尺寸的皮卡，價格會是多少，甚至不確定它是燃油車還是電動車。不過，有幾種可能性值得思考。

Firstly, VW could just start building the Amarok in the US. That’s a truck designed for South America, but Ford’s Ranger was designed in Australia for non-US markets, and it’s now on sale here, so that approach could work. That sure would be boring, though.

首先，大眾可以開始在美國生產 Amarok。那是為南美設計的卡車，但福特的 Ranger 最初也是在澳洲為非美國市場設計的，現在也在美國銷售，所以這種方法可能行得通。不過，那樣肯定會很無聊。

For a more considered alternative, there’s Scout, the new VW Group brand for premium off-road-focused pickups and SUVs. In Michigan, Scout is designing vehicles to be built in South Carolina, using a body-on-frame platform with either battery electric or range-extended powertrains. Those are slated to arrive in 2028. If VW decides to enter the full-size segment and compete against Ford’s F-150 and the other heavy hitters, a body-on-frame platform would be necessary to get the performance that the market expects.

一個更周全的替代方案是 Scout，這是大眾集團專為高端越野皮卡和 SUV 創立的新品牌。在密西根州，Scout 正在設計將在南卡羅萊納州製造的車輛，採用非承載式車身平台，並配備純電動或增程式動力系統。這些車輛預計於 2028 年上市。如果大眾決定進入全尺寸級距並與福特的 F-150 等強手競爭，非承載式車身平台將是獲得市場預期性能的必要條件。

Whether that can be done with profit margins high enough to justify the effort depends on the numbers, including whether a VW truck on the same platform would cannibalize too many Scout sales.

是否能以足夠高的利潤率來支撐這項努力取決於數據，包括在相同平台上掛大眾品牌的卡車是否會蠶食過多 Scout 的銷量。

A more interesting alternative also involves platform sharing, but it shouldn’t steal any sales from Scout. In recent weeks, we’ve seen a ~$25,000 electric pickup from Slate Auto, and next year, the electric Ford Fathom is arriving at less than $30,000. What if VW decided that two is a coincidence but three is a trend and that small, cheap trucks are actually the way to electrify America?

另一個更有趣的替代方案也涉及平台共享，但它不應該搶走 Scout 的任何銷量。最近幾週，我們看到了來自 Slate Auto 售價約 25,000 美元的電動皮卡，而明年，售價低於 30,000 美元的電動福特 Fathom 也將上市。如果大眾認為出現兩次是巧合，但出現三次就是趨勢，而小型廉價卡車實際上是讓美國電動化的途徑呢？

Developing its own low-cost electric truck from scratch by 2030 is a tall order. VW could build one using its MEB platform, as used by its mainstream brands; that’s what underpins the VW ID Buzz and ID.4. And the ID.4 recently ended production at VW’s factory in Chattanooga, Tennessee, so it should have capacity for this putative pickup.

在 2030 年前從零開始開發自己的低成本電動卡車是一項艱鉅的任務。大眾可以利用其 MEB 平台來製造一輛，就像其主流品牌所使用的一樣；這也是福斯 ID Buzz 和 ID.4 的基礎。而 ID.4 最近剛在田納西州查塔努加的大眾工廠停產，因此那裡應該有能力生產這款假想的皮卡。

Ford licensed MEB; VW can license UEV

福特授權了 MEB；大眾也可以授權 UEV

Basing it on MEB is one way to go, but what if VW decided to license a platform from another automaker? Like Ford.

以 MEB 為基礎是一種方法，但如果大眾決定從另一家汽車製造商那裡授權平台呢？例如福特。

In Europe, Ford does it the other way, borrowing the MEB platform to build electric Ford Capris and Ford Explorers. European Ford fans are as aghast at the misappropriation of the Capri name as ‘Merican Mustang fans were over the Mach-E’s moniker, but as a traveler from these shores, when I see one drive past in Spain, Germany, or the UK, I’m mostly just sad we don’t get them this side of the Atlantic Ocean.

在歐洲，福特則是反向操作，借用 MEB 平台來打造電動 Ford Capri 和 Ford Explorer。歐洲的福特迷對 Capri 這個名字被誤用感到震驚，就像美國野馬迷對 Mach-E 的取名一樣，但作為一名來自此地的旅人，當我在西班牙、德國或英國看到一輛車駛過時，我大多只是覺得難過，因為我們在大西洋的這一端買不到它們。

So what if VW licensed Ford’s new Universal Electric Vehicle platform? For the last few years, a small team has been working independently of the rest of Ford to develop a lower-cost EV architecture from scratch.

那麼，如果大眾授權了福特新的通用電動車（UEV）平台會怎樣？在過去幾年裡，一個小團隊一直獨立於福特其他部門，致力於從零開始開發一種成本較低的電動車架構。

Ford’s first EVs were technologically solid, but seemingly endless price hikes didn’t help the Mach-E or F-150 Lightning. Maybe trying to get EV adoption going in the US via the full-size pickup was the wrong approach. It’s certainly a huge segment of the market, and one that’s effectively limited to US OEMs or rivals willing to build those trucks locally. That’s thanks to a protectionist law with decades of staying power that should give companies still contemplating exporting cars from China to the US pause. But pickup buyers really, really care about specs, and if the sheet says your truck can’t tow as much as your buddy’s V8-powered pickup or that you’ll have to stop three times as often on the way to the lake with your boat, forget about it.

福特的首批電動車在技術上很紮實，但似乎永無止境的漲價對 Mach-E 或 F-150 Lightning 沒有幫助。也許試圖透過全尺寸皮卡在美國推廣電動車是錯誤的方法。這確實是市場的一個巨大區塊，而且實際上僅限於美國本土廠商或願意在當地生產這些卡車的競爭對手。這要歸功於一項擁有數十年生命力的保護主義法律，它應該能讓那些仍在考慮從中國向美國出口汽車的公司停下來思考。但皮卡買家真的非常、非常看重規格，如果數據表上說你的卡車拖曳能力不如你朋友的 V8 引擎皮卡，或者你在帶著船去湖邊的路上得停下來充電三次，那就別想了。

That’s why the electric F-150 replacement has been delayed in favor of something smaller and more affordable. That truck, the Fathom, is due next year, but perhaps VW could license its UEV platform for an electric truck of its own? That even raises the tantalizing possibility of exporting some back to Europe in bright yellow paint and Skoda badges—Felicia Fun EV, anyone?

這就是為什麼電動 F-150 的替代車型被推遲，轉而優先開發更小、更實惠的車型。那款卡車 Fathom 預計明年上市，但也許大眾可以授權其 UEV 平台來生產自己的電動卡車？這甚至開啟了一個誘人的可能性：將其中一些漆成鮮黃色並掛上 Skoda 標誌出口回歐洲——有人想來輛 Felicia Fun EV 嗎？

整篇摘要

福斯汽車（Volkswagen）計畫在 2030 年前於美國生產皮卡貨車，以挽救其在北美下滑的業績。由於福斯目前缺席了高利潤的皮卡與大型 SUV 市場，報導指出該公司正考慮透過自有技術或授權福特平台來填補此缺口。此舉旨在應對全球銷量衰退與內部裁員壓力，並針對美國消費者對載重與性能的特殊偏好進行產品轉型。
