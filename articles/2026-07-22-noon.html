China’s AI models have Trump’s AI world at war with itself

中國的 AI 模型讓川普的 AI 圈陷入內鬥

Last weekend, several current and former advisers to President Donald Trump on AI publicly lobbed insults at the country’s leading AI companies. David Sacks branded Anthropic’s models “lobotomized” and “woke.” Emil Michael, a top Pentagon official, called OpenAI’s new head of strategic futures a “supreme village idiot.”

上週末，幾位現任及前任川普的 AI 顧問公開羞辱該國領先的 AI 公司。David Sacks 稱 Anthropic 的模型為「被切除腦葉的」且「覺醒（woke）」。前五角大廈官員 Emil Michael 稱 OpenAI 的新任策略未來負責人為「終極村中白痴」。

It began because no one can agree on what to do about Kimi, a free, open-source model that Chinese AI company Moonshot launched last week. It appears to rival the intelligence of models from OpenAI and Anthropic, which are very much not free.

這起爭議源於大家對如何處理 Kimi 意見不一。Kimi 是中國 AI 公司月之暗面（Moonshot）上週推出的免費開源模型。它的智慧程度似乎足以與 OpenAI 和 Anthropic 的模型匹敵，而後兩者的模型並非免費。

Every time a new smart, free model from China gets released, US companies see less reason to fork out money for models from Anthropic or OpenAI. That’s creating economic and political problems for the president—and dividing the top AI strategists in his orbit.

每當中國發布新的智慧、免費模型時，美國企業就越沒有動力花錢購買 Anthropic 或 OpenAI 的模型。這給總統帶來了經濟與政治問題，並使他周圍的頂尖 AI 策略家產生分歧。

Inside Chicago’s surveillance panopticon

深入芝加哥的監控全景監獄

Early on the morning of September 2, 2024, four people were shot and killed on a westbound train in Chicago. Police swiftly activated a digital dragnet—a surveillance network that connects thousands of cameras across the city—and arrested the suspect just 90 minutes later.

2024 年 9 月 2 日清晨，四人在芝加哥一列往西行駛的火車上遭槍擊身亡。警方迅速啟動了數位搜捕網——一個連接全市數千台攝影機的監控網絡——並在短短 90 分鐘後逮捕了嫌疑人。

Law enforcement and security advocates say this vast monitoring system protects public safety and works well. But activists and many residents say it's a surveillance panopticon that creates a chilling effect on behavior and violates guarantees of privacy and free speech.

執法部門和安全倡導者表示，這個龐大的監測系統保護了公共安全且運作良好。但活動人士和許多居民表示，這是一個全景監獄（panopticon），對行為產生了寒蟬效應，並違反了隱私和言論自由的保障。
