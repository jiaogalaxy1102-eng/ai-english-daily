Anthropic’s newest commercial begins with a sharp piano note, and what sounds like recordings of people asking tough questions of our current moment navigating artificial intelligence.

Anthropic 最新的廣告以清脆的鋼琴聲開場，聽起來像是人們針對當前引領人工智慧發展的關鍵時刻所提出的種種艱難問題的錄音。

“Can AI be trusted? Who’s going to hit the brakes if we need to? How do we really ensure what we’re aiming to achieve really does benefit the majority of people? If it ends up taking almost all of the jobs, then what does it mean to work?”

「AI 值得信任嗎？如果我們需要踩煞車，誰來負責？我們如何確保我們致力實現的目標真的能造福大多數人？如果它最終取代了幾乎所有的工作，那麼工作的意義又是什麼？」

Then about halfway through, the questions shift to reflect a more optimistic point of view. “Could AI help people stop feeling misunderstood? Could AI help me build more connections in the community? Can AI help me be a better teacher and a better mom? Maybe it will cure some great things, things we’re not even at the cusp of understanding yet. . . . What if we started to be more human again?”

接著大約到了一半，問題轉向反映出更樂觀的觀點。「AI 能幫助人們不再感到被誤解嗎？AI 能幫我在社區建立更多連結嗎？AI 能幫我成為更好的老師和更好的媽媽嗎？也許它會治癒一些偉大的事物，那些我們甚至還未觸及理解邊緣的事物。...如果我們開始再次變得更有『人性』呢？」

Created by the ad agency Mother London, the spot is a clear attempt to walk the fine line we’re all walking when it comes to AI and our own use of large language models (LLMs). It’s asking tough questions about trust, job loss, and societal impact, then juxtaposing it with more inspirational questions about how this technology may not be our end but a salvation.

這支由廣告代理商 Mother London 創作的短片，顯然是試圖在我們面對 AI 及使用大型語言模型（LLM）時所處的微妙平衡中尋求共識。它提出了關於信任、失業和社會影響的艱難問題，隨後將其與更具啟發性的問題並列，探討這項技術可能不是我們的終結，而是救贖。

These are compelling questions, and ones we should all ask ourselves every time we turn to ChatGPT or Claude for a recipe, fitness advice, or detailed character breakdown of WKRP in Cincinnati. But the fact it’s coming from one of the companies leading us down this road at breakneck speed is feeling a bit rich for many. As one Instagram commenter wrote, “This ad attempts to shift to optimism in the second half and dupe people in its slick messaging, but what people don’t realize is that the gloom and doom from the first half is Claude’s business model. . . .”

這些都是引人入勝的問題，也是我們每次轉向 ChatGPT 或 Claude 尋求食譜、健身建議或《WKRP in Cincinnati》詳細角色分析時都該自問的問題。但對於許多人來說，這些話出自正以驚人速度引領我們走向這條路的其中一家公司之口，顯得有些虛偽。正如一位 Instagram 評論者所寫：「這則廣告試圖在後半段轉向樂觀，並以圓滑的訊息欺騙大眾，但人們沒有意識到，前半段那些陰鬱與毀滅正是 Claude 的商業模式...」

In May, Anthropic’s CEO, Dario Amodei, warned that artificial intelligence has created a narrow window for the world’s tech firms, governments, and banks to fix tens of thousands of software vulnerabilities found by his company’s latest model Mythos. Then in a June blog post, the company wrote, “We believe it would be good for the world to have the option to slow or temporarily pause frontier AI development to enable societal structures and alignment research to keep up with the advance of the technology.”

今年五月，Anthropic 的執行長 Dario Amodei 警告說，人工智慧為全球科技公司、政府和銀行創造了一個狹窄的窗口，以修復其公司最新模型 Mythos 發現的數萬個軟體漏洞。隨後在六月的一篇部落格文章中，該公司寫道：「我們認為，如果世界能有選擇放慢或暫時暫停前沿 AI 的開發，以使社會結構和對齊研究能趕上技術進步，這對世界將是有益的。」

This puts these new AI brands in a Catch-22 dilemma. Make an ad too fun and it trivializes all these serious issues. Make it too self-serious and you’re accused of being disingenuous. This is not a problem advertising alone can fix.

這讓這些新的 AI 品牌陷入了進退兩難的困境。把廣告做得太有趣，會淡化這些嚴肅問題的重要性；做得太過嚴肅，又會被指責為虛偽。這不是單靠廣告就能解決的問題。
