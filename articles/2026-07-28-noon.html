This influential industry event brought together leaders and innovators to discuss our creative and technological futures, and the importance of keeping designers at the centre of the conversations.

這次具有影響力的產業活動聚集了領導者與創新者，共同探討我們創意與科技的未來，以及將設計師置於對話中心的重要性。

From the clean-lined worlds of Tintin and the Smurfs to the boundary-pushing music of Angèle and Stromae, Belgium has a rich history of storytelling which has made an impact the world over. Today, this legacy is expanding within Southern Belgium’s burgeoning cultural hub, Wallonia-Brussels: a territory emerging as a hub for the intertwining of design, technology and creative entrepreneurship.

從丁丁和藍色小精靈那線條簡潔的世界，到安琪兒（Angèle）與司徒邁（Stromae）挑戰極限的音樂，比利時擁有深厚的敘事歷史，並對全球產生了影響。如今，這份傳承正在比利時南部蓬勃發展的文化中心——瓦隆-布魯塞爾擴張：該地區正成為設計、科技與創意創業相互交織的樞紐。

Across audiovisual production, fashion, architecture, music, performance and immersive media, the region’s creative industries are being shaped by collaboration as much as craft. After all, Belgium is home to three official languages and communities from around 180 nationalities, so cultural overlap is embedded – and Wallonia-Brussels has become a place where cultural exchange naturally feeds experimentation, resulting in projects that often blur the boundaries between art, technology and experience.

跨越影音製作、時尚、建築、音樂、表演和沉浸式媒體，該地區的創意產業正受到協作與工藝的共同形塑。畢竟，比利時擁有三種官方語言以及來自約 180 個國家的社群，文化交融已根植其中——而瓦隆-布魯塞爾已成為一個文化交流自然孕育實驗的地方，產出的計畫往往模糊了藝術、科技與體驗之間的界限。

That growing ecosystem was the focus of Belgium House 2026, which returned to London’s Shoreditch Arts Club following its inaugural edition last year. Organised by Wallonia-Brussels International, the event brought together creatives, cultural organisations, researchers and industry leaders from Belgium and the UK for a day of conversations exploring immersive storytelling, creative technologies, music, design and cross-border collaboration.

這種不斷成長的生態系統是 2026 年比利時館（Belgium House 2026）的焦點。該活動在去年首屆舉辦後，再次回歸倫敦的肖迪奇藝術俱樂部。由瓦隆-布魯塞爾國際組織，該活動匯集了來自比利時和英國的創意人、文化組織、研究人員和產業領袖，進行為期一天的對話，探索沉浸式敘事、創意科技、音樂、設計和跨境合作。

Rather than simply showcasing Belgian talent, Belgium House served to connect creative communities and promote discussion around the topics currently shaping Europe’s cultural industries: how immersive technologies can move beyond novelty, how storytelling evolves through ipation">participation, and how designers can continue to influence technological development as new tools emerge. Part of the discussions were representatives from initiatives such as Kikk Festival, XR4Europe and Ikii studio, which have been integral to building the ecosystem of artists, researchers and creative entrepreneurs across the Wallonia-Brussels region. Imagine a world where Wallonia-Brussels is a European creative tech valley – that is the future these initiatives, and others like Wake! By Digital Wallonia, are working towards.

比利時館不僅僅是展示比利時的人才，更致力於連結創意社群，並推動有關當前塑造歐洲文化產業話題的討論：沉浸式技術如何超越新鮮感、敘事如何透過參與演變，以及隨著新工具的出現，設計師如何繼續影響技術開發。參與討論的代表來自 Kikk Festival、XR4Europe 和 Ikii studio 等倡議組織，這些組織對於建立瓦隆-布魯塞爾地區的藝術家、研究人員和創意企業家生態系統至關重要。想像一個瓦隆-布魯塞爾成為歐洲創意科技谷的世界——這正是這些倡議以及像 Wake! By Digital Wallonia 等組織正努力邁向的未來。

“Belgium is setting the pace for Europe’s next creative wave,” says Motonobu Kasajima, general representative of Wallonia-Brussels to the UK. “From three major awards at Cannes to Belgian designers shaping the vision of the world’s leading fashion houses, to the unmistakable voices of Angèle and Stromae resonating across continents, our artists are proving that creativity knows no borders.”

「比利時正在為歐洲的下一波創意浪潮設定節奏，」瓦隆-布魯塞爾駐英國總代表 Motonobu Kasajima 表示。「從在坎城獲得的三項大獎，到塑造全球頂尖時尚屋願景的比利時設計師，再到安琪兒和司徒邁響徹各洲的獨特嗓音，我們的藝術家正在證明創意無國界。」

“Belgium may be a small country,” adds Belgian ambassador to the UK Jeroen Cooreman, “but it’s a country of remarkable creativity. From Tomorrowland, which has become one of the world's most iconic music festivals, to EVS, whose live video production technology helps bring major international events such as the Olympic Games, the UEFA Champions League, Wimbledon and Formula 1 to audiences around the world, Belgian innovation and talent continue to make an impact far beyond our region.”

「比利時或許是個小國，」比利時駐英國大使 Jeroen Cooreman 補充道，「但它是一個擁有非凡創造力的國家。從已成為全球最具代表性音樂節之一的 Tomorrowland，到其現場視訊製作技術協助將奧運、歐冠聯賽、溫布頓和 F1 等重大國際賽事帶給全球觀眾的 EVS，比利時的創新與人才持續在我們地區之外產生影響。」

From lab to impact: translating immersive research into real-world adoption

從實驗室到影響力：將沉浸式研究轉化為現實世界的應用

The opening panel explored how immersive research can make the leap from innovation labs into everyday cultural experiences. Bringing together speakers from XR4Europe, Digital Catapult and Eirmersive, the conversation centred on collaboration across Europe, digital sovereignty and the role immersive technologies can play across sectors ranging from culture to healthcare. According to Camille Donegan (CEO of Eirmersive and director of XR4Europe), what bridges the gap between research and adoption is the core idea that “narrative-driven experiences stick. We resonate with other humans.”

開場小組討論探討了沉浸式研究如何從創新實驗室躍進到日常文化體驗中。匯集了來自 XR4Europe、Digital Catapult 和 Eirmersive 的講者，對話集中在歐洲各地的合作、數位主權，以及沉浸式技術在從文化到醫療保健等各個領域所扮演的角色。根據 Eirmersive 執行長兼 XR4Europe 總監 Camille Donegan 的說法，橋接研究與應用之間的鴻溝之核心概念是：「敘事驅動的體驗會留下深刻印象。我們與他人產生共鳴。」

From screen to stepping inside: turning global IP into living worlds

從螢幕到走入其中：將全球 IP 轉化為生動的世界

The next discussion segued into fandom, participatory experiences and the future of storytelling as an elevated experience. Among the speakers was Etienne Sainton from Belgian experience design company Alterface, whose work with Mycotoo on Stranger Things: The Experience demonstrates how interactive design can transform familiar intellectual property into physical environments. Dawn Foot, from Katapult and TEA EME Division, shared a few eye-opening statistics on immersive experiences: that 65 per cent of people imagine themselves in a TV show and 72 per cent of people want more enjoyment in their lives – reinforcing the growing appetite for immersive experiences that feel personal rather than passive.

接下來的討論轉向粉絲文化、參與式體驗以及敘事作為一種提升體驗的未來。講者包括來自比利時體驗設計公司 Alterface 的 Etienne Sainton，他與 Mycotoo 在《怪奇物語：體驗》中的合作，展示了互動設計如何將熟悉的知識產權轉化為實體環境。來自 Katapult 和 TEA EME 分部的 Dawn Foot 分享了一些關於沉浸式體驗的驚人數據：65% 的人想像自己身處電視節目中，72% 的人希望生活中能有更多樂趣——這加深了人們對感受到個人化而非被動沉浸式體驗的日益增長的渴望。

Beyond Van Gogh: rethinking the future of immersive experiences

超越梵谷：重新思考沉浸式體驗的未來

The final panel explored how immersive exhibitions can be revealing and meaningful experiences rather than ones driven purely by spectacle. Moderated by Marie du Chastel of Kikk Festival and Ikii studio, the discussion questioned how commercial investment is shaping the sector, and whether experiences driven primarily by return on investment leave enough room for artistic risk. “What do we lose in terms of diversity, boldness and artistic vision?” du Chastel asked, a question that resonated far beyond immersive exhibitions alone.

最後的小組討論探討了沉浸式展覽如何成為具啟發性且有意義的體驗，而非純粹由奇觀驅動。由 Kikk Festival 和 Ikii studio 的 Marie du Chastel 主持，討論質疑了商業投資如何塑造該產業，以及主要由投資報酬率驅動的體驗是否為藝術風險留下了足夠空間。「我們在多樣性、大膽程度和藝術視野方面失去了什麼？」du Chastel 問道，這個問題引發的共鳴遠不止於沉浸式展覽本身。

Kanal-Centre Pompidou

卡納爾-龐畢度中心

Belgium House also offered attendees an early look at one of Brussels’ biggest cultural developments. Set to open in November 2026, Kanal-Centre Pompidou will transform a former Citroën factory into one of Europe’s largest museums dedicated to modern and contemporary art, housing works on loan from Paris’ Centre Pompidou while establishing its own programme rooted in Brussels’ cultural landscape.

比利時館也讓與會者得以搶先一睹布魯塞爾最大的文化發展項目之一。卡納爾-龐畢度中心預計於 2026 年 11 月開幕，將把前雪鐵龍工廠轉變為歐洲最大的現代與當代藝術博物館之一，館內將展出從巴黎龐畢度中心租借的作品，同時建立紮根於布魯塞爾文化景觀的自有計畫。

Taken together, the conversations at Belgium House reflected a region thinking beyond individual creative disciplines. Whether through immersive technology, exhibition-making or music, the focus remained on creating meaningful connections between people, ideas and industries.

總結來說，比利時館的對話反映了一個超越單一創意學科的地區。無論是透過沉浸式技術、展覽製作還是音樂，焦點始終在於建立人、想法與產業之間的有意義連結。

As Jeroen Cooreman put it, the event was ultimately about “bringing together creative minds, innovators, entrepreneurs and decision-makers to exchange ideas, build new partnerships and strengthen the ties between Belgium and the United Kingdom” – and, in turn, making a case for creative collaboration across Europe.

正如 Jeroen Cooreman 所言，這次活動最終目的是「匯聚創意人才、創新者、企業家和決策者，交流想法、建立新的合作夥伴關係並加強比利時與英國之間的聯繫」——進而為全歐洲的創意協作提供實證。

Wallonia-Brussels presents Belgium House 2026 - the one-day creative collision bringing together influential innovators across art, media, tech, and science to transform the Shoreditch Arts Club into a hub of immersive innovation, cutting-edge talks, and meaningful networking.

瓦隆-布魯塞爾呈現 2026 比利時館——這場為期一天的創意碰撞匯集了藝術、媒體、科技和科學領域具影響力的創新者，將肖迪奇藝術俱樂部轉變為沉浸式創新、前沿演講和有意義社交的中心。
