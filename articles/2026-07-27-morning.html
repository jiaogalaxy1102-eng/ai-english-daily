There’s something about a movie like The Room, Troll 2, or Fateful Findings that I find irresistible. These sorts of “so bad they’re good” films are marvelous curiosities where ambition far outstrips resources, ability, and self-awareness to become something much greater than the sum of their parts. Champagne and Bullets (also released as GetEven and Road to Revenge) is a film on par with those classics of outsider cinema.

像是《房間》、《巨魔2》或《命運的發現》這類電影總有些讓我無法抗拒的地方。這些所謂「爛到極點反而好看」的電影是奇妙的珍品，其野心遠超資源、能力和自覺，最終成就了遠大於各部分總和的作品。《香檳與子彈》（也以《GetEven》和《復仇之路》為名發行）是一部足以與那些非主流電影經典並駕齊驅的作品。

The movie is almost entirely the work of one man, John De Hart. Hart wrote, directed, and stars as Rick Bode in the film. He even performs a musical number early in the proceedings — The bizarre “Shimmy Slide.” During the performance, Hart, who was reportedly pumped full of painkillers after throwing out his back, is stiff and looks around the room as if he’s only performing under threat of violence. And somehow, this lifeless performance of a cringy country rocker isn’t the most awkward scene in the movie.

這部電影幾乎全是約翰·德·哈特一個人的作品。哈特編劇、導演，並在片中飾演瑞克·博德。他甚至在劇情早期表演了一段歌舞——詭異的〈Shimmy Slide〉。在表演過程中，據報導哈特在閃到腰後被灌滿了止痛藥，顯得僵硬，並環視房間，彷彿是在暴力威脅之下才進行表演。而不知何故，這段由令人尷尬的鄉村搖滾樂手帶來的死氣沉沉的表演，竟然還不是電影中最尷尬的場景。

The relationships and interactions between characters in Champagne and Bullets are just as inexplicable and unnatural as those in The Room. Bode and his partner Huck Finney are cops who lose their badges five minutes into the movie. This precipitates the alarmingly quick mental decline of Finney, who is also going through a divorce after discovering his wife has been sleeping around. He fires a handgun through his bills, drinks bleach to get out of prison, and seemingly starts a cult.

《香檳與子彈》中角色間的關係與互動，就和《房間》裡的一樣令人費解且不自然。博德和他的夥伴哈克·芬尼是警察，在電影開演五分鐘後就丟了警徽。這促使芬尼的心理狀態急劇惡化，而在發現妻子偷情後，他也正經歷離婚。他對著帳單開槍，喝漂白水以逃獄，甚至似乎創立了一個邪教。

Oh, and speaking of cults, a good portion of the plot revolves around the reemergence of Bode’s old girlfriend Cindy, who is fleeing a satanic coven that “sacrificed a human baby.”

喔，說到邪教，劇情中有很大一部分圍繞著博德的前女友辛蒂的再次出現，她正在逃離一個「犧牲人類嬰兒」的撒旦祕密結社。

While ostensibly an action film, the fight scenes are few and far between, and staged with all the believability of a theme park stunt show. Hart seemed far more interested in cramming in as much gratuitous nudity as possible. There are three uncomfortably long sex scenes with costar Pamela Bryant, who plays Cindy, all set to songs sung by Hart. In total, they take up 12 minutes of runtime in a movie that is only 90 minutes long. One scene that takes place in a jacuzzi apparently even led Hart’s girlfriend at the time to break up with him. (Warning: One scene not involving Hart and Bryant does depict a sexual assault.)

雖然表面上是一部動作片，但動作場面寥寥無幾，且呈現出的可信度就像主題樂園的特技秀一樣。哈特似乎對塞進盡可能多的無謂裸戲更感興趣。片中有三段與飾演辛蒂的搭檔帕梅拉·布萊恩特進行、長得令人不適的性愛場面，配樂全是哈特唱的歌。總共在僅 90 分鐘長的電影中佔據了 12 分鐘。其中一段發生在按摩浴缸的戲，顯然甚至導致哈特當時的女友跟他分手。（警告：一段不涉及哈特和布萊恩特的場景確實描繪了性侵犯。）

If you’re a fan of bad movies, cult classics, or the podcast How Did This Get Made, you owe it to yourself to go watch Champagne and Bullets. It’s available on Hoopla, Amazon Prime, and Tubi.

如果你是爛片、邪典經典或播客節目《這到底是怎麼拍出來的》的粉絲，你一定要去看看《香檳與子彈》。它在 Hoopla、Amazon Prime 和 Tubi 上都有提供。
