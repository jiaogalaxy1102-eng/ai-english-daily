An island quarantine, but it’s in outer space

島嶼隔離，但它位於外太空

Moxley, Frederick I. & Ricciardi, Anthony. “Protecting earth from extraterrestrial contamination: The case for a lunar biocontainment facility.” Ambio.

Moxley, Frederick I. & Ricciardi, Anthony. 〈保護地球免受外星污染：建立月球生物封鎖設施的案例〉。《Ambio》。

Well, folks: It’s finally time to build a quarantine on the Moon for aliens.

各位，終於到了在月球上為外星人建造檢疫所的時候了。

That’s the upshot of a study published in May that argues for the establishment of “a lunar biocontainment facility” to house extraterrestrial samples en route to Earth until they are deemed safe.

這是五月份發表的一項研究的結論，該研究主張建立一個「月球生物封鎖設施」，用來存放運往地球途中的外星樣本，直到它們被判定為安全為止。

The U.S. and China's space programs both aim to bring back samples from the surface of Mars in the coming years, and many missions have already delivered rocks from asteroids. The new study anticipates a disastrous scenario in which samples from nearby celestial bodies might contain dangerous extraterrestrial life.

美國和中國的太空計畫都目標在未來幾年內從火星表面帶回樣本，而且許多任務已經送回了來自小行星的岩石。這項新研究預見了一種災難性的情況：來自鄰近天體的樣本可能含有危險的外星生命。

“If samples from Mars or other celestial bodies harbor dormant or active biological entities, then Earth’s biosphere could become exposed to a potentially invasive novel organism with unpredictable pathogenicity or ecological effects,” said Frederick I. Moxley of Strategic Threat and Analysis Research Laboratories, LLC, and Anthony Ricciardi of McGill University.

Strategic Threat and Analysis Research Laboratories, LLC 的 Frederick I. Moxley 和麥基爾大學的 Anthony Ricciardi 表示：「如果來自火星或其他天體的樣本帶有休眠或活躍的生物體，那麼地球的生物圈可能會暴露在一種具有不可預測致病性或生態影響的潛在入侵性新型生物中。」

“For example, if extraterrestrial life exists, its molecular architecture could reflect atypical biochemistry (such as chirality reversal or alternative nucleic acid analogs) that is hazardous to Earth’s ecosystems and even to human biology,” the team added.

團隊補充道：「例如，如果外星生命存在，其分子結構可能反映出非典型生物化學（如手性反轉或替代核酸模擬物），這對地球生態系統甚至人類生物學都是有害的。」

The team pointed to earthly analogs, such as viral strains that have broken containment or species that thrive in habitats that are completely different from the ones in which they originally evolved. If life on Earth can be this adaptable and unpredictable, why should we expect anything different from life elsewhere?

團隊指出了一些地球上的類比，例如突破封鎖的病毒株，或在與其原始演化環境完全不同的棲息地中茁壯成長的物種。如果地球上的生命可以如此具適應性且不可預測，我們為什麼要期待其他地方的生命會有所不同？

“The Moon must not merely serve as a stepping stone for exploration, but also as a preemptive shield between Earth and potential forms of extraterrestrial contamination…ensuring that exploration does not come at the expense of Earth’s biosphere” the team said. “The formalization of such precautionary lunar quarantine protocols will not only safeguard our planet, but also affirm humanity’s maturity as an interplanetary civilization.”

團隊表示：「月球不應僅僅作為探索的跳板，還應作為地球與潛在形式外星污染之間的預防性屏障……確保探索不會以犧牲地球生物圈為代價。這種正式的預防性月球檢疫協議不僅將保護我們的星球，也將肯定人類作為星際文明的成熟度。」

Moreover, “the economic cost for a lunar biocontainment facility is negligible compared to the potential ecological devastation of an invasive extraterrestrial organism.”

此外，「月球生物封鎖設施的經濟成本與外星入侵生物可能帶來的生態毀滅相比，是微不足道的。」

There you have it: We can’t afford not to build an alien Moon quarantine.

這就是結果：我們承擔不起「不」建造外星月球檢疫所的代價。

Axes of ancients

古人的斧頭

Barkai, Ran and Shalata, Muataz. “Lower Palaeolithic Tools of Potency: Handaxes Shaped around Fossils and Other Extraordinary Features at Sakhnin Valley, Israel.” Tel Aviv.

Barkai, Ran 和 Shalata, Muataz. 〈下舊石器時代的潛能工具：以色列薩赫寧谷以化石和其他非凡特徵塑形的手斧〉。《特拉維夫》。

Some 500,000 years ago, early humans collected fossils and geodes and crafted them into handaxes, according to a study published in March that suggested these decorative tools had symbolic significance to their makers.

根據三月份發表的一項研究，大約 50 萬年前，早期人類收集化石和晶洞，並將其製作成手斧，這表明這些裝飾性工具對其製造者具有象徵意義。

From 2024 to 2025, archaeologists discovered an unprecedented trove of ten fossil-bearing handaxes in what is today the Sakhnin Valley in Israel, which were made by the influential early human Homo erectus. While previous digs have unearthed the occasional handaxe containing fossils, this is the first site to yield a whole hoard of them.

從 2024 年到 2025 年，考古學家在現今以色列的薩赫寧谷發現了前所未有的十件含化石手斧珍藏，這些工具是由具有影響力的早期人類「直立人」製作的。雖然之前的挖掘偶爾會發現含有化石的手斧，但這是第一個出土整批此類樣本的遺址。

“To our knowledge, no other archaeological context besides Sakhnin Valley has yielded more than a single extraordinary specimen,” said authors Ran Barkai of Tel Aviv University and Muataz Shalala, a local independent researcher who found the site.

特拉維夫大學的作者 Ran Barkai 和發現該遺址的當地獨立研究員 Muataz Shalala 表示：「據我們所知，除了薩赫寧谷之外，沒有其他考古背景曾出土超過一件以上的特殊標本。」

“We propose that these handaxes served as tools and mediators between humans and the cosmos, conceived as objects of potency enhanced by the primeval fossil imprints and unique geological features within the stone,” the team said. “The richly embellished rocky landscape of Sakhnin Valley, particularly with its abundant geodes, may have encouraged early humans to express their profound and extraordinary relationships with the cosmos via stones.”

團隊表示：「我們提議，這些手斧是人類與宇宙之間的工具和媒介，被構思為透過原始化石印記和石頭內獨特地質特徵增強力量的物件。薩赫寧谷豐富綴飾的岩石景觀，尤其是其豐富的晶洞，可能鼓勵了早期人類透過石頭表達他們與宇宙之間深厚且非凡的關係。」

The human appreciation for cool rocks knows no bounds. Given that there’s no obvious functional advantage to the fossil-bearing stones beyond aesthetics, It’s tantalizing to imagine what these geological rarities meant to the bygone humans who selected them.

人類對酷炫石頭的喜愛是無止境的。鑑於這些含有化石的石頭除了美學之外沒有明顯的功能優勢，想像這些地質稀有物對那些挑選它們的已逝人類意味著什麼，著實令人心癢。

Go with the (sperm) flow

隨著（精子）流動

Alsous, Jasmin Imran, Chakrabarti, Brato, et al. “The physical consequences of sperm gigantism.” Nature Physics.

Alsous, Jasmin Imran, Chakrabarti, Brato 等人. 〈精子巨大症的物理後果〉。《自然物理》。

Here’s a fun fact to share at a party: Fruit flies have the longest sperm in nature. Measuring anywhere from several millimeters to more than two inches depending on the species, these bugs offer an extreme example of “sperm gigantism,” a phenomenon seen across many animal species.

這裡有一個適合在派對上分享的冷知識：果蠅擁有自然界中最長的精子。測量長度從幾毫米到超過兩英吋不等（取決於物種），這些昆蟲提供了「精子巨大症」的一個極端例子，這種現象在許多動物物種中都能見到。

Now, scientists have probed the mystery of how these “ultralong sperm” avoid getting all tangled up in their journey through the female reproductive tract, according to a study published in June.

根據六月份發表的一項研究，現在科學家們探究了這些「超長精子」在穿過雌性生殖道的過程中，如何避免糾纏在一起的神祕機制。

Using in vivo imagery and mathematical models, researchers revealed how two-millimeter-long sperm from the fruit fly Drosophila melanogaster autonomously organizes themselves en route to prospective fertilization.

研究人員利用活體影像和數學模型，揭示了黑腹果蠅兩毫米長的精子如何在前往預期受精的途中自主組織起來。

“Giant sperm have evolved in several animal taxa for >100 million years—a testament to the enduring success of sperm gigantism as a sexual strategy,” said researchers co-led by Jasmin Imran Alsous of the Flatiron Institute and Brato Chakrabarti of International Centre for Theoretical Sciences in India.

由熨斗研究所的 Jasmin Imran Alsous 和印度國際理論科學中心的 Brato Chakrabarti 共同領導的研究小組表示：「巨大精子在幾種動物分類群中已經演化了超過一億年——這證明了精子巨大症作為一種生殖策略的持久成功。」

Fruit fly sperm seamlessly forms “collective flows” that are “primarily associated with the dense packing of sperm, rather than the specific geometry of the confining organs,” the team found.

團隊發現，果蠅精子無縫地形成了「集體流動」，這「主要與精子的密集包裝有關，而非受限器官的特定幾何形狀」。

Who knew fruit fly sperm could be so mesmerizing? Nature continues to astonish in the most unexpected places.

誰能想到果蠅的精子竟然如此迷人？大自然在最意想不到的地方持續讓人驚嘆。

Venus gets a cosmic glow-up

金星獲得宇宙級的美化

Karyu, Hiroki et al. “A cosmic origin of Venus’ lower haze.” Nature Astronomy.

Karyu, Hiroki 等人. 〈金星低層霾霧的宇宙起源〉。《自然天文》。

For more than a half-century, scientists have pondered the origin of the so-called “lower haze” on Venus, a particle-rich layer of clouds at about 20 to 30 miles of altitude that was first detected by spacecraft in the 1970s.

半個多世紀以來，科學家們一直在思考金星上所謂「低層霾霧」的起源，這是 1970 年代首次被太空船探測到、位於海拔約 20 到 30 英里處的多粒子雲層。

In April, a team finally solved the mystery; turns out it’s just a light sprinkling of cosmic dust from bygone shooting stars that broke up and dispersed in the Venusian skies. In addition to providing an ethereal explanation for the phenomenon on Venus, the new results may also inform our understanding of exoplanet atmospheres.

今年四月，一個團隊終於解開了這個謎團；事實證明，那只是來自過去流星碎裂並散佈在金星天空中的宇宙塵埃的輕微灑落。除了為金星上的現象提供優雅的解釋外，新結果還可能增進我們對系外行星大氣層的理解。

“On planets like Earth, where surface liquid water exists, rain constantly scavenges involatile particles from the air,” said researchers led by Hiroki Karyu of Tohoku University. “However, as observed in our Solar System, planets with surface liquid water are truly unique; most planets possess dry atmospheres where liquid droplets evaporate before reaching the surface.”

由東北大學的 Hiroki Karyu 領導的研究小組表示：「在像地球這樣表面存在液態水的行星上，降雨會不斷地清除空氣中的不揮發顆粒。然而，正如在我們太陽系中所觀察到的，擁有表面液態水的行星確實是獨一無二的；大多數行星擁有乾燥的大氣層，液滴在到達表面之前就會蒸發。」

“In such worlds, the lower haze originating from cosmic dust can accumulate in the hot lower atmosphere, enhancing cloud formation” and “substantially [impacting] the planet’s climate,” the team added. “These effects establish cosmic dust as an essential component of planetary climates, a role that is also likely to be important for exoplanets.”

團隊補充說：「在這樣的世界中，源自宇宙塵埃的低層霾霧會積聚在熾熱的低層大氣中，增強雲層的形成並顯著影響行星的氣候。這些效應確立了宇宙塵埃作為行星氣候的重要組成部分，這一角色對於系外行星可能同樣重要。」

No big deal, Venus is just marinating in cosmic crumbs that can shed light on alien skies. Go on with your day.

沒什麼大不了的，金星只是浸泡在可以照亮外星天空的宇宙碎屑中。請繼續過你的日子吧。
