The illustrator reveals how repetition and patience shapes her tiny illustrations, which transform everyday moments into tactile paper artworks.

這位插畫家揭示了重複與耐心如何塑造她的微型插畫，將日常瞬間轉化為具觸感的紙藝作品。

For South Korean illustrator and artist Makitoy, the act of drawing sits closer to “recording time than producing a final result”, she tells us. The artist’s practice consists of persistent observation and repetition through daily collages and illustrations. She charts whole months with micro collages the size of stamps and whole years with zoomed-in snapshots of nature graphically translated into paper through the use of various drawing tools.

對於南韓插畫家兼藝術家 Makitoy 而言，繪畫的行為與其說是產出最終結果，「更接近於記錄時間」，她告訴我們。這位藝術家的實作包含透過每日拼貼與插畫進行持續的觀察與重複。她以郵票大小的微型拼貼記錄整個月份，並透過多種繪圖工具，將放大的自然快照圖形化地轉化為紙藝，以此記錄整年。

“Plants are one of the most important subjects in my practice. The way they slowly change and grow in their own rhythm closely reflects my way of working,” she says. The artist’s focus on this subject sees her transform natural shapes into minimal lines and structures. These reduced graphic constructions allow her to explore subtle interactions of colours, “the space between forms, the flow of light and the quiet sense of order that appears when unnecessary elements are removed,” she says.

「植物是我實作中最重要的主題之一。它們以自己的節奏緩慢變化與生長的方式，密切地反映了我的工作方式，」她說。藝術家對此主題的關注，讓她將自然形狀轉化為極簡的線條與結構。這些簡化的圖形構造讓她得以探索色彩之間微妙的互動，「形式之間的空間、光影的流動，以及去除不必要元素後顯現的安靜秩序感，」她說。

More recently the artist has expanded on these drawings of nature with a series of still life works, capturing objects like watering cans, armchairs and paint brushes all with just a few colourful shards of paper. “I aim to transform ordinary forms and moments into visual fragments that carry warmth,” the illustrator says. “I am particularly drawn to the quiet atmosphere carried by aged objects.” By cutting and sticking these colourful moments from everyday life down, Makitoy is observing even the most subtle shifts in their daily responses to their own visual world – changes that appear in the form and colour of anything we choose to depict over time. The simple, restrained beauty of the results certainly make the case for drawing to be treated as a daily ritual.

最近，藝術家進一步擴展了這些自然畫作，推出了一系列靜物作品，僅用幾片彩色的紙片就捕捉了澆水壺、扶手椅和畫筆等物品。「我的目標是將平凡的形式與瞬間轉化為帶有溫度的視覺碎片，」插畫家說。「我特別被老舊物品所承載的安靜氛圍所吸引。」透過剪貼這些日常生活中色彩繽紛的時刻，Makitoy 觀察著她對自身視覺世界日常反應中最細微的轉變——這些變化隨著時間出現在我們選擇描繪的任何事物的形式與色彩中。這些結果簡約且克制的美感，無疑為將繪畫視為一種每日儀式提供了充分的理由。
