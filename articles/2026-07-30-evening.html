The architects behind planned Kansas City Chiefs stadium want it to be as noisy as possible

The Kansas City Chiefs were taking on the New England Patriots on September 29, 2014, when, with just eight seconds to go in the first quarter, the volume of the crowd’s roar reached 142.2 decibels. That’s about as loud as a jet engine taking off, and good enough to break the Guinness World Record for loudest crowd roar at a sports stadium.

2014年9月29日，堪薩斯城酋長隊正在對陣新英格蘭愛國者隊，就在第一節僅剩八秒時，現場群眾的歡呼聲量達到了142.2分貝。這大約相當於噴射機引擎起飛時的音量，足以打破體育場內最響亮群眾歡呼聲的金氏世界紀錄。

A new Chiefs stadium might help them break it again.

一座新的酋長隊體育場可能會幫助他們再次打破這項紀錄。

The Chiefs previously set the record for loudest crowd in 2013 at 137.5 decibels, which was broken later that year when the crowd at Seattle’s CenturyLink Field reached 137.6 decibels. But since 2014’s game, when the Chiefs handed out earplugs to fans beforehand, the record has remained untouched.

酋長隊先前在2013年創下了137.5分貝的最響亮群眾紀錄，但該紀錄在同年晚些時候被西雅圖世紀互聯體育場（CenturyLink Field）的群眾以137.6分貝打破。但自2014年的那場比賽（當時酋長隊事先向球迷發放了耳塞）以來，該紀錄一直未被動搖。

When the team’s planned $3 billion stadium opens in 2031, though, that record could be at risk again. The lead architectural firm behind the stadium says it is designed to be as loud as possible by containing the sound through the actual size, shape, and roof geometry of the venue.

然而，當球隊計畫耗資30億美元的體育場於2031年啟用時，該紀錄可能會再次受到威脅。負責該體育場的主要建築事務所表示，其設計旨在透過場館的實際尺寸、形狀和屋頂幾何結構來鎖住聲音，從而達到盡可能大的音量。

“We carefully designed the layout of the seating environment, the roof, and the concourse areas to contain the sound inside the seating environment, much more than the existing Arrowhead stadium does today,” David Manica, owner of Manica Architecture, tells Fast Company.

Manica Architecture 的負責人 David Manica 告訴《Fast Company》：「我們精心設計了座位環境、屋頂和廣場區域的佈局，將聲音鎖在座位區域內，這比現有的箭頭體育場（Arrowhead stadium）做得更多。」

Manica, the Kansas City-based firm behind venues like Allegiant Stadium in Las Vegas and the Oklahoma City Arena in Oklahoma City, designed the stadium, and HNTB, another Kansas City firm, is the lead design architect.

Manica 是這家總部位於堪薩斯城的公司，曾設計過拉斯維加斯的忠誠體育場（Allegiant Stadium）和奧克拉荷馬城的奧克拉荷馬城體育館（Oklahoma City Arena），負責該體育場的設計；而另一家堪薩斯城公司 HNTB 則是首席設計建築師。

The team says the stadium was designed to prioritize fan experiences like tailgating, with parking lot infrastructure big enough to hold more than 20,000 parking spots, and the pre-game flyover, by using 400,000 square feet of translucent panels for a clear view through its fully enclosed roof. When it comes to maxing out the volume inside the stadium, the entire venue’s shape was optimized for loudness.

球隊表示，體育場的設計優先考慮了賽前派對等球迷體驗，其停車場基礎設施規模足以容納超過20,000個車位，並透過使用40萬平方英尺的半透明面板，讓全封閉式屋頂下也能清晰看到賽前的飛機衝場表演。當涉及到將體育場內的音量最大化時，整個場館的形狀都針對響度進行了優化。
