He’s hard at work on the upcoming Beat Gems, a gorgeous coffee-table book about the history of drum machines.

他正忙於即將出版的《Beat Gems》，這是一本關於鼓機歷史的華麗精裝書。

Oli Freke is a musician and journalist whose works have appeared in Sound on Sound, The Quietus, and Mixmag. This has included using math to explore the melodic potential of the Western 12-tone scale and deep dives on effects plug-ins. He’s even written a book tracing the evolution of the synthesizer from 1963 through 1995, charting the change from analog to digital and back.

Oli Freke 是一位音樂家兼記者，其作品曾發表於《Sound on Sound》、《The Quietus》和《Mixmag》。這包括利用數學探索西方十二音階的旋律潛力，以及對音效外掛程式的深入探討。他甚至寫過一本追溯合成器從 1963 年到 1995 年演進過程的書，記錄了從類比到數位再轉回類比的變遷。

His new project is Beat Gems, a coffee-table visual history of drum machines from publisher Bjooks. Like other Bjooks titles, Beat Gems is loaded with gorgeous photos, fascinating historical tidbits, and practical advice for making the most of your gear, whether it’s a vintage Roland TR-808 or a modern oddity like Erica Synths’ Pērkons.

他的新計畫是《Beat Gems》，這是一本由 Bjooks 出版社發行的鼓機視覺歷史精裝書。就像其他 Bjooks 的書目一樣，《Beat Gems》收錄了華麗的照片、引人入勝的歷史趣聞，以及充分發揮設備性能的實用建議，無論是復古的 Roland TR-808 還是像 Erica Synths 的 Pērkons 這樣的現代奇特產品。

What is the first app you install on a new phone or computer?

你在新手機或電腦上安裝的第一個應用程式是什麼？

If I’m buying a new computer, it’ll almost certainly be my music computer, so I’ll be installing Ableton Live straight away. It’s a remarkable piece of music software, with a feature set that still feels staggering compared to when I started making electronic music with an Atari ST 520 that could just about manage MIDI, with no audio at all.

如果我買了一台新電腦，那幾乎肯定會是我的音樂電腦，所以我會立即安裝 Ableton Live。這是一款非凡的音樂軟體，與我剛開始用只能勉強處理 MIDI 且完全沒有音頻的 Atari ST 520 製作電子音樂時相比，它的功能集依然令人驚嘆。

Which social media platform do you use the most (if any)?

你最常使用哪個社群媒體平台（如果有的話）？

Probably YouTube, if we’re counting it as social media. It’s brilliant having experts in almost any subject area explain ideas, concepts, and methods. I watch a lot of science, maths, chess, and, inevitably, electronic music. I could do with fewer algorithmic clickbait temptations, which is one reason I’m not a heavy user of other social media platforms.

如果是把 YouTube 也算作社群媒體的話，大概就是它了。讓幾乎任何領域的專家來解釋想法、概念和方法是很棒的一件事。我看了很多科學、數學、西洋棋，還有不可避免地，電子音樂。我希望能少一點演算法帶來的點擊誘餌誘惑，這也是我不常使用其他社群媒體平台的原因之一。

Which is the most disappointing gadget you’ve ever owned?

你擁有過最令人失望的裝置是什麼？

Vegetable choppers and cubers seem like a great idea, but they never work properly and never actually save any time or effort.

切菜機和切丁機聽起來是個好主意，但它們從來沒有正常運作過，也從未真正節省過任何時間或精力。

What game do you have the fondest memories of?

哪款遊戲讓你擁有最美好的回憶？

Elite on the Atari ST was incredible. It’s world-building on a (memory) shoestring when severe technical constraints existed, and the results were truly creative.

Atari ST 上的《Elite》非常不可思議。這是在存在嚴重技術限制的時代，以極少的內存進行的世界建構，結果非常具有創意。

What is one thing you wish you had created?

有什麼是你希望自己創造出來的？

As a musician, I wish I’d written a song that the whole world knew and loved, and that fans would demand I play at every opportunity — until I got so heartily sick of it that, in the end, I wished I’d never written it. Perhaps this has happened, and all these wishes have come true!

作為一名音樂家，我希望我寫過一首全世界都知道並喜愛的歌，讓歌迷在每個場合都要求我演奏——直到我對它感到由衷地厭倦，以至於最後希望自己從未寫過它。也許這已經發生了，所有這些願望都成真了！

What’s the best piece of advice you’ve ever received?

你收到的最好建議是什麼？

From my father (I’m appropriately writing this on Father’s Day in the UK): “Say yes to any opportunity that comes your way — worry about whether you can do it or not later!”

來自我的父親（寫這段話時剛好是英國的父親節）：「對任何降臨到你身上的機會說好——晚點再擔心你能不能做到！」

What is your current obsession?

你目前最著迷的事物是什麼？

Drum machines! Researching for the Beat Gems book has reminded me of the primacy of rhythm to the human musical experience; how drum machines have tapped into this need, and in doing so have transformed musical culture over the last sixty years. It’s utterly fascinating.

鼓機！為《Beat Gems》這本書做的研究提醒了我節奏在人類音樂體驗中的首要地位；鼓機如何利用了這種需求，並在過去六十年間改變了音樂文化。這絕對引人入勝。

What do you do when you need to focus?

當你需要專注時，你會做什麼？

I put on www.rinse.fm/kool, choose a set of banging drum’n’bass rollers, and then I’m very happy (and focused).

我會打開 www.rinse.fm/kool，選擇一組強勁的 Drum’n’Bass 舞曲，然後我就會感到非常愉快（且專注）。

What do you do when you’re feeling stuck?

當你感到陷入瓶頸時，你會做什麼？

Remind myself of the infinite number of combinations of ideas. I once wrote an article that demonstrated there were 82 quintillion (82^18) 10-note melodies that can be written with the eight notes of the octave.

提醒自己想法有無窮無盡的組合。我曾經寫過一篇文章，證明用八個音階可以寫出 820 萬兆（82^18）種十音符旋律。

What’s the last piece of physical media you bought?

你買的最後一件實體媒體是什麼？

The soundtrack on vinyl to a beautiful film called Resynator. It’s about an obscure synth from the early ’80s and a daughter’s quest to learn about her father, who invented it but sadly died while she was a baby. Can’t recommend it enough.

一部名為《Resynator》的美麗電影的黑膠原聲帶。它是關於 80 年代初期的一款冷門合成器，以及一個女兒尋找關於她父親的故事，她的父親發明了這款合成器，卻遺憾地在她還是嬰兒時去世了。非常推薦這部片。

What do you think is worth splurging on?

你認為什麼值得大肆花錢購買？

A high-quality road bicycle. Compared with cars, bikes are cheap, and you can buy machines not far off the quality professionals use for relatively reasonable sums. The difference between a good bike and a run-of-the-mill one is vast. Cycling in the countryside on a good bike brings me a great deal of joy.

高品質的公路單車。與汽車相比，單車很便宜，而且你可以用相對合理的金額買到與專業選手使用的品質相差無幾的機器。好單車和普通單車之間的差異是巨大的。騎著好單車在鄉間穿梭帶給我極大的快樂。

What would the tagline for your biopic be?

你的傳記電影標語會是什麼？

Watch a man type words into a computer. For ages. Then watch him make music on a computer. Also for ages. Then watch him do some normal things involving a house, family, and car. Occasionally he is turned away from boarding a plane to Berlin to go to the launch of his Kickstarter because they changed the rules without him realizing it! Disappointingly, this doesn’t transform the biopic into a Falling Down-type scenario.

看一個男人在電腦上打字，打了很久。然後看他在電腦上做音樂，也做了很久。接著看他做一些涉及房子、家庭和汽車的平常事。偶爾，他會因為航空公司在沒通知他的情況下更改了規則，而被拒絕登上前往柏林參加他 Kickstarter 發表會的飛機！令人失望的是，這並沒有讓傳記電影轉變成像《城市英雄》那樣的戲劇性情節。
