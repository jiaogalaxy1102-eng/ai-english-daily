D-Wave is a bit of an oddity in the quantum computing space, having been founded back in the last century. And its initial offering wasn’t a quantum computer like those being developed by IBM or Google. Instead, the company built what’s now called a quantum annealer, a machine that isn’t general-purpose but can solve a large class of optimization problems. While the hardware shares some similarities with the qubits used in gate-based quantum computers, it operates in a fundamentally different way.

D-Wave 在量子運算領域算是一個異類，早在上個世紀就已成立。而且它最初提供的並非像 IBM 或 Google 正在開發的那種量子電腦。相反地，該公司建造了現在被稱為「量子退火機」的設備，這種機器並非通用型，但可以解決一大類優化問題。雖然其硬體與門檻式量子電腦中使用的位元有一些相似之處，但它的運作方式根本上完全不同。

But a few years back, D-Wave started working on gate-based hardware, apparently choosing a somewhat unusual qubit technology called fluxonium. And this year, the company acquired a startup called Quantum Circuits that spun out of Yale University and has been developing what’s called a dual-rail qubit (the same technology used by Amazon), which promises to make most errors very easy to detect, simplifying error correction.

但幾年前，D-Wave 開始研發門檻式硬體，顯然選擇了一種較為罕見的位元技術，稱為「磁通量位元（fluxonium）」。而今年，該公司收購了一家名為 Quantum Circuits 的新創公司，該公司是從耶魯大學獨立出來的，一直致力於研發所謂的「雙軌位元」（與 Amazon 使用的技術相同），這種技術有望使大多數錯誤變得非常容易偵測，進而簡化糾錯過程。

On Wednesday, the company is publishing a paper in Nature that describes a key step in validating this dual-rail technology, showing that two of the qubits can be entangled without altering their best feature: Most are a single type that is easy to detect.

週三，該公司在《Nature》上發表了一篇論文，描述了驗證這種雙軌技術的關鍵步驟，展示了兩個位元可以在不改變其最佳特性的情況下進行糾纏：大多數錯誤都是易於偵測的單一類型。

The basic structure of a dual-rail qubit depends on making two linked resonators, which we’ll call left and right. If you place a single photon in the system and measure it, it will always be in either the left or right resonator. But it’s possible to place that single photon in a superposition of both left and right. Those are all the features you need to make a qubit.

雙軌位元的基本結構取決於製造兩個連結的共振器，我們稱之為左和右。如果你在系統中放入單個光子並進行測量，它將始終處於左側或右側共振器中。但也可以將該單個光子置於左右兩側的疊加狀態。這些就是製造量子位元所需的全部特性。

The nice thing about the dual-rail setup is that the most common error is simply the photon escaping the hardware. (This is sometimes referred to as an “erasure qubit,” as the loss of the photon erases the information it contains.) The next most common error is flipping the phase of the qubit, with bit flips being a very distant third. Crucially, photon loss can be easily detected with the right hardware, without needing additional qubits required to run an error-correction code.

雙軌設置的好處是，最常見的錯誤僅僅是光子逃逸硬體。（這有時被稱為「抹除位元」，因為光子的流失會抹除其包含的資訊。）次常見的錯誤是翻轉位元的相位，而位元翻轉則遠遠排在第三位。至關重要的是，利用正確的硬體可以輕鬆偵測到光子流失，而不需要運行糾錯碼所需的額外位元。

You’ll still need an error code to handle those other errors. But since they should be less frequent and phase flips will be far more common, it should be possible to use a simpler, more compact error code—one that can potentially be tailored to the relative frequency of the remaining two error types. That means you need to devote fewer hardware qubits to each logical, error-corrected qubit. D-Wave is betting that this will allow it to get useful quantum computation without having to build as much hardware.

你仍然需要糾錯碼來處理其他錯誤。但由於它們發生的頻率較低，且相位翻轉會常見得多，因此應該可以使用更簡單、更精簡的糾錯碼——一種可以根據剩餘兩種錯誤類型的相對頻率量身定制的代碼。這意味著你只需要為每個邏輯糾錯位元投入較少的硬體位元。D-Wave 賭注這將使其能夠在不必建造過多硬體的情況下，獲得有用的量子運算。

But dual-rail qubits aren’t as widely studied as many other approaches, which is why the company is publishing a paper today. The relative frequencies of the three types of errors have primarily been studied in single qubits that are simply sitting still, trying to maintain their initial value. Performing computations will require a lot of operations on qubits, and it’s possible that those operations could have a different hierarchy of errors.

但雙軌位元並不像其他許多方法那樣被廣泛研究，這就是該公司今天發表論文的原因。這三種錯誤類型的相對頻率主要是在靜止不動、試圖維持初始值的單個位元中研究的。執行運算將需要對位元進行大量操作，而這些操作可能會產生不同的錯誤層級。

“Often when you start producing two qubit or entangling gates, you can distort your error hierarchy,” D-Wave’s Trevor Lanting told Ars. “So for dominant errors, things other than erasures can start cropping in. One of the fundamental things that we’re very excited about with this two-qubit gate [that] we’re publishing in Nature is that it preserves the error hierarchy.”

D-Wave 的 Trevor Lanting 告訴 Ars：「通常當你開始產生兩個位元或糾纏閘時，你可能會扭曲你的錯誤層級。因此對於主要錯誤，除了抹除之外的其他東西可能會開始出現。我們對這篇發表在《Nature》上的雙位元閘感到非常興奮，其中一個根本原因是它保留了錯誤層級。」

(For the curious, Amazon hasn’t needed to do something similar because it chose a system where any connections between dual-rail qubits are mediated by an intervening qubit based on the transmon. As a result, it never has to directly entangle dual-rail qubits.)

（給好奇的人：Amazon 不需要做類似的事情，因為它選擇了一種系統，其中雙軌位元之間的任何連接都是透過基於 transmon 的中間位元來調解的。結果，它永遠不必直接糾纏雙軌位元。）

D-Wave set out to do three things in the paper: demonstrate entanglement of two dual-rail qubits, show that the operation was fast enough for practical quantum computing, and show that any errors occurred in roughly the same proportion as they do when using the qubit as memory.

D-Wave 在論文中致力於完成三件事：演示兩個雙軌位元的糾纏，證明操作速度快到足以進行實際的量子運算，並顯示任何發生的錯誤比例與將位元用作記憶體時大致相同。

The company set up a pair of dual-rail qubits, each containing two coupled resonators. Between them was a tunable coupler that could be switched on or off. Depending on the state of this coupler, the two qubits would either operate independently or interact. Practically, this involves setting the coupler so that one qubit (termed the control qubit) partially occupies the coupler, allowing it to interact with the second.

該公司設置了一對雙軌位元，每個位元包含兩個耦合共振器。它們之間是一個可以開啟或關閉的可調耦合器。根據這個耦合器的狀態，這兩個位元既可以獨立運行，也可以相互作用。實際上，這涉及設置耦合器，使一個位元（稱為控制位元）部分佔用耦合器，使其能夠與第二個位元相互作用。

Running an entangling gate turned out to be extremely simple: Activate the connection, wait, and then shut it off again. The “wait” portion would not even give you time to check your watch; it’s about 200 nanoseconds, with the entire entanglement taking 500 ns. “We can do these operations in a few hundred nanoseconds, so these are fast operations,” Lanting said. “So not only do you have kind of the high fidelity of the dual rail devices, but you are producing fast entangling operations.”

運行糾纏閘被證明極其簡單：啟動連接，等待，然後再次關閉。這個「等待」部分甚至不會給你時間看錶；大約是 200 奈秒，整個糾纏過程耗時 500 奈秒。Lanting 說：「我們可以在幾百奈秒內完成這些操作，所以這些是快速的操作。因此，你不僅擁有雙軌設備的高保真度，而且還產生了快速的糾纏操作。」

Critically, it had the same hierarchy of error types. Photon loss rates were about 0.5 percent per entanglement, and they were five times more common than any other type of error. “Bit-flips are practically non-existent at the 10−6 level,” the paper says. Most of the losses affected the source qubit, which is also allowed to partially enter the coupler.

至關重要的是，它具有相同的錯誤類型層級。每次糾纏的光子流失率約為 0.5%，且其發生頻率是任何其他類型錯誤的五倍。論文指出：「位元翻轉在 10⁻⁶ 水平上幾乎不存在。」大部分損失影響了來源位元，該位元也被允許部分進入耦合器。

“The addition of a fast and high-fidelity entangling operation completes the toolbox of gates and operations for dual-rail cavity qubits,” Lanting said.

Lanting 說：「快速且高保真糾纏操作的加入，完善了雙軌腔位元的運算閘與操作工具箱。」

That doesn’t mean D-Wave’s work is done, of course. To begin with, the devices showed rising error rates as more operations were done. “As the number of gates increases, both the fidelity and purity of the final state exhibit an unexpected approximately quadratic decrease,” the paper acknowledges. “We propose the cause to be drift in calibration parameters or fluctuations in the frequency of the coupling transmon on timescales of experimental shot averaging.”

當然，這並不意味著 D-Wave 的工作已經完成。首先，隨著操作次數增加，設備顯示出錯誤率上升。「隨著閘數量的增加，最終狀態的保真度和純度都呈現出出乎意料的約二次方下降，」論文承認。「我們認為原因在於校準參數的漂移，或是在實驗次數平均時間尺度上耦合 transmon 頻率的波動。」

So there’s more work to do before the system is ready for more complex operations.

因此，在系統準備好進行更複雜的操作之前，還有更多工作要做。

Error correction also requires that the hardware support measurements while a calculation is in progress, which a number of other technologies have already demonstrated. That’s still a work in progress for photon-loss detection. “We are working on techniques for doing mid-circuit erasure detection,” Lanting said. “We don’t have this perfected yet, but we’ve got ideas and a plan and a roadmap for detecting these, not just at the end but as you’re running your circuit.”

糾錯還要求硬體支援計算過程中的測量，這是一些其他技術已經演示過的。對於光子流失偵測來說，這仍在研發中。Lanting 說：「我們正在研究執行電路中抹除偵測的技術。我們還沒有完善這一點，但我們有了想法、計畫和路線圖，不僅在結束時，而且在運行電路時偵測這些錯誤。」

The other area that needs significant work is the theory of error detection when there are such large differences between classes of errors. The classical compute needed to interpret the error data—called the syndrome—of a logical qubit is substantial. But Lanting said he expects it can be reduced by the dual-rail qubit, providing what he termed a “richer set of information in the data stream that’s flowing off the [quantum processor].”

另一個需要重大工作的領域是當各類錯誤之間存在如此巨大差異時的糾錯理論。解釋邏輯位元的錯誤數據（稱為症狀）所需的經典運算量非常龐大。但 Lanting 表示，他預計雙軌位元可以減少這種運算量，提供他所謂的「從量子處理器流出的數據流中更豐富的信息集」。

There’s time to work that out. D-Wave plans to reach 181 dual-rail qubits by 2028, which should be enough time to test several forms of the surface code error-detection scheme. Should that hardware perform as expected, the challenge will then be to make and link enough of it to host a hundred or more logical qubits.

還有時間解決這個問題。D-Wave 計畫到 2028 年達到 181 個雙軌位元，這應該有足夠的時間來測試幾種形式的面碼糾錯方案。如果該硬體表現如預期，接下來的挑戰將是製造並連結足夠的硬體，以承載一百個或更多邏輯位元。
