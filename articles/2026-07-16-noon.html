Chinese-Taiwanese artist co-founder of It’s Happening Angela Lian joined us on our New York Nicer Tuesdays stage in June – and she made it very clear that one thing is a constant through all of her work: pain. The artist centres chronic pain and illness in her work (like many designers, she’s always dealing with posture issues and back pains!) and she delivered a brilliant talk on how her work exercises not just her creative muscles but her body’s too, revealing how her design work at Baggu brings together the clear-minded and the beautifully printed.

中台裔藝術家暨 It’s Happening 共同創辦人 Angela Lian，於六月登上了我們在紐約舉辦的 Nicer Tuesdays 舞台，她非常明確地表示，有一件事貫穿了她所有的作品：疼痛。這位藝術家將慢性疼痛與疾病置於其創作核心（就像許多設計師一樣，她總是得應對姿勢不良和背痛的問題！）。她發表了一場精彩的演講，內容關於她的工作如何不僅鍛鍊她的創意肌肉，也鍛鍊她的身體，並揭示了她在 Baggu 的設計工作如何將思緒清明與精美的印刷結合在一起。
